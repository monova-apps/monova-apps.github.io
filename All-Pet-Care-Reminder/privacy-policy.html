1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to All Pet Care Reminder (the “Application”), package org.mypetspal.petpal, operated by Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. (“we,” “us,” “our,” or the “Service Provider”).

It explains information handling associated with pet-care records, reminders, advertisements, premium features, and communications with us. On-device processing is distinguished from information transmitted to us or another organisation.

Where applicable law uses the term “data controller,” we act as controller for processing whose purposes and means we determine. An external provider's responsibilities depend on its actual role and contractual arrangements.

2. Information We Handle

Pet profiles, reminders, and care records

The Application handles information you enter into pet profiles, care reminders, completion history, and notes. Care entries can concern feeding, walks, grooming, medication schedules, vaccinations, appointments, refills, and other tasks you choose to record.

Although an entry concerns a pet, it can also identify a person or reveal household routines. Notes and attachments may include an owner's contact information, a veterinary contact, an address, or other personal details. Include only what is necessary for your records.

Photos and associated information

Images you choose to provide may contain identifiable people, documents, location clues, and embedded metadata. Adding an image does not automatically remove that information or make the image anonymous.

The published Data safety declaration lists photos and videos, app activity, and device or other identifiers among the categories the Application may collect. These broad categories do not, by themselves, establish which files are uploaded, where they are stored, or which provider receives them.

Complete the data mapping: Identify the exact profile fields, media, activity events, and identifiers processed; distinguish local access from transmission; and identify the destination, purpose, and retention arrangements for each category. Do not describe all pet records as local-only without checking the implementation.

Support communications

If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, attachments, and ordinary email delivery information. You may voluntarily include your device model, Android version, or app version to explain an issue.

Remove unrelated private information from screenshots. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, identity documents, or an entire care history when a shorter explanation is sufficient.

3. Reminders and Device Permissions

Reminder notifications

Reminder features use the schedule and details you configure to provide alerts. Android notification permission and device settings can determine whether those alerts are displayed.

Notification previews may reveal a pet's name, care task, or other displayed details to someone viewing your screen. Review your lock-screen settings when reminders contain private information.

Disabling notifications does not automatically delete the underlying reminder or care record.

Photos and camera access

The permissions needed for adding an image depend on the implemented feature and Android version. A system photo picker can grant access to selected items without granting access to an entire library. A direct camera-capture feature, if implemented, has different access requirements.

The precise photo-access method and any camera functionality must be verified for this Application. A declaration covering photos and videos does not prove that the app records video or reads every image.

Other access

An in-app calendar view does not, by itself, establish access to your device's personal calendars. Similarly, a walking reminder does not establish GPS tracking. Any such access must be separately identified if used.

You can review available permissions in Android settings. Revoking access can affect the relevant feature but does not necessarily erase information already copied or transmitted.

4. Advertising, Measurement, and Analytics

The Application contains advertisements. Advertising services may communicate directly with their providers through integrated software development kits, commonly called SDKs. That processing is part of the Application's disclosures even when we do not receive a copy on our own servers.

Information involved in advertising

Depending on the deployed SDK and its settings, advertising processing can involve IP addresses, approximate location inferred from an IP address, advertising or installation identifiers, device and app information, ad impressions, interactions, diagnostics, and privacy-choice records.

These are implementation-dependent examples, not a verified inventory for this Application. Relevant purposes can include ad delivery, performance measurement, fraud prevention, and personalisation where implemented and legally permitted.

Advertising-provider disclosure to complete: Identify the actual advertising network, all relevant mediation partners, and any analytics or crash-reporting providers. Add their official privacy-policy links, information received, processing purposes, retention arrangements, and privacy controls.

Confirm whether pet records, uploaded media, reminder text, or precise location are accessible to or passed to these providers. Do not assume either that this occurs or that these categories are excluded.

Consent and personalisation

Where applicable law requires consent before advertising-related device access or processing, that activity must not begin before valid consent is obtained. The published policy must explain the actual controls for refusing or withdrawing that consent.

Non-personalised advertising does not necessarily mean that no information is processed. Delivery, measurement, and security functions may still use information, depending on the provider and configuration.

Selecting an advertisement may open another website, application, or store page. Information you independently provide there is handled by the relevant recipient. This does not remove our responsibility for SDK processing within the Application.

5. Premium Features and Purchase Information

The published app description offers premium features, including expanded usage and an ad-free experience. Premium access may require processing a record of the entitlement associated with a purchase.

Where Google Play Billing is used, purchase handling can involve product identifiers, purchase tokens, transaction status, and entitlement verification. The exact billing provider, server verification, and information retained by this Application must be identified before publication.

An ad-free entitlement should not be interpreted as automatic deletion of previously collected advertising information or proof that all other SDK activity has stopped.

Cancelling a subscription, requesting deletion of personal information, and uninstalling the app are different actions. Use the relevant purchase provider's controls to manage a subscription.

6. Purposes and Legal Grounds

Care information is used to provide the record and reminder functions you select. Advertising and purchase information must be used only for the verified purposes described for those services.

Information you send us is used to respond to communications, investigate reported issues, handle privacy requests, and satisfy applicable legal obligations. Where necessary and lawful, it may also be used to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Where a legal basis is required, the final policy must identify it for each relevant purpose. Consent, contractual necessity, legal obligations, and legitimate interests are not interchangeable. Any reliance on legitimate interests must identify the actual interest and account for users' rights.

You may withdraw consent for processing that relies on it without affecting earlier lawful processing. Installing the app, granting notification permission, or continuing to use it does not substitute for separate affirmative consent where required.

7. When Information Is Disclosed

Relevant recipients may include the verified advertising, billing, hosting, and other service providers used by the Application, together with providers handling communications you send us. These advertising controls do not necessarily remove ads, erase previously collected records, or replace privacy controls required within the Application.

Clearing the cache alone is not the same as clearing all app data. Local deletion does not automatically delete remotely stored information, backups, purchase records, or information already held by advertising providers.
