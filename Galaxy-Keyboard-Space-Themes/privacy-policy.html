This Privacy Policy applies to Galaxy Keyboard: Space Themes (the “Application”), operated by Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd., Birtamod Municipality-4, Jhapa, Nepal (“we”, “us”, or “our”). The Application provides an Android keyboard with galaxy and space-inspired appearance options.

2. Keyboard Input and Sensitive Information

When enabled and selected, the Application processes key presses, composed text, and editing commands and delivers the resulting input to the active app and field through Android.

Android may expose limited editor information, including input type, cursor position, selected or surrounding text, and the app requesting input. Access depends on Android and the editor and does not provide unrestricted access to all apps, messages, or historical activity.

We do not retain a complete typing history, upload typed content or surrounding text, or disclose them to advertising services. The destination app determines how it processes text after receiving it.

A keyboard necessarily handles entered characters and may encounter passwords, payment details, or private messages. We do not separately collect or retain that content as a record. Password-field behaviour depends partly on how the destination app identifies its fields; this policy does not guarantee technical inability to encounter sensitive content.

3. Local Preferences and Optional Features

Theme selection, available visual effects, layout, language, size, sound, vibration, and other preferences may be saved locally. Space-themed visual effects do not require access to your physical location.

If suggestions or a personal dictionary are included, processing and supported learning remain on-device. The workflow described here does not use cloud prediction. Learned words can contain personal information and can be removed using available reset controls or app-data deletion.

If clipboard features are available, clipboard text is accessed for actions you request under Android’s restrictions. Supported saved clips remain local until removed. Clipboard content is not supplied to advertisers.

If custom backgrounds are supported, selected images are used locally for personalisation. App-specific copies may persist; removing them does not necessarily remove the original image.

4. Permissions and Activation

You activate the keyboard by enabling and selecting it in Android input-method settings. Installation alone does not activate it. Android may show a general warning about third-party keyboard access.

Android’s BIND_INPUT_METHOD protection restricts connections to the keyboard service. It is not broad permission to read contacts, messages, or unrelated files.

Network and advertising-related access supports AdMob. An image picker or media permission may support an included custom-background feature. The typing workflow does not require camera access, microphone recording, contacts, SMS, call history, or precise GPS. A separate voice-input service, if invoked, operates under its provider’s permissions and policy.
