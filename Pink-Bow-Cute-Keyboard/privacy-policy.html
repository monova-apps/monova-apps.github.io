1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to Pink Bow Cute Keyboard (the “Application”), operated by Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd., Birtamod Municipality-4, Jhapa, Nepal (“we”, “us”, or “our”). The Application provides an Android keyboard with pink, bow-inspired appearance options.

This policy explains keyboard access, local preferences, advertising, support communications, and privacy choices. Optional features apply only when included in your release. We act as controller for personal information whose purposes and means of processing we determine.

2. Keyboard Input and Sensitive Information

When enabled and selected, the keyboard processes key presses, composed text, and editing commands and delivers input to the active app and field through Android.

Android may make limited editor information available, including field type, cursor position, selected or surrounding text, and the app requesting input. This depends on Android and the receiving editor. It does not provide unrestricted access to all apps or their historical contents.

We do not maintain a complete typing history, transmit typed content or editor text to our servers, or provide it to advertising services. The destination app independently handles text after receiving it.

A keyboard can encounter sensitive input, including passwords, payment information, and private messages. We do not separately collect or retain this content as a record. Sensitive-field behaviour depends partly on how the destination app marks its fields; this policy does not guarantee that such content is technically inaccessible to the input method.

3. Local Preferences and Optional Features

Available theme, keyboard size, layout, language, sound, vibration, and display preferences may be stored locally. Appearance choices are not used by us to infer your gender, age, or other sensitive characteristics.

If suggestions or a personal dictionary are included, processing and any supported learning remain on-device. No cloud prediction service is used in the workflow described here. Learned words may contain personal information and can be removed using available controls or app-data deletion.

If clipboard features are included, clipboard text is accessed for actions you request under Android’s restrictions. Supported saved clips remain local until removed. Clipboard content is not supplied to advertisers.

If custom backgrounds are supported, images you select are processed locally for keyboard personalisation. An app-specific copy may remain until removed. The original image has a separate storage lifecycle.

4. Permissions and Activation

You enable and select the keyboard in Android input-method settings. Installation alone does not activate it. Android may show a general warning about the potential access of third-party keyboards.

Android’s BIND_INPUT_METHOD protection controls connections to the keyboard service. It does not grant unrelated access to contacts, messages, or device files.

Network and advertising-related access supports AdMob. An image picker or media permission may support an included custom-background feature. The typing workflow does not require camera, microphone recording, contacts, SMS, call history, or precise GPS. If you invoke a separate voice-input service, its provider’s permissions and policy apply.
