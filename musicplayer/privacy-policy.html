1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to Music player play your music (the “Application”), operated by Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. (“we,” “us,” “our,” or the “Service Provider”).

It explains how information is accessed, used, stored, and disclosed when you use the Application in the configuration described above or communicate with us. It distinguishes information processed on your device from information received by the Service Provider.

Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. is responsible for personal information we receive and determine how to use, including support correspondence and privacy requests. Where applicable data-protection law uses the term “controller,” we act as controller for that processing.

2. Information We Handle

Music files and media information

To display and play music that you make available to the Application, the local-player configuration accesses the relevant audio files and associated media information. Depending on the file, that information can include its title, artist, album, duration, file name, file location, and embedded artwork.

This access is used for music browsing and playback. Reading a music file on your device is not the same as uploading it. In the configuration covered by this policy, your audio files and music-library information are not uploaded to servers operated by us or to remote music-analysis services.

Audio files can contain personal information, including information in recordings or their metadata. We do not treat files as anonymous merely because they are described as music or audio.

Playback information and preferences

The Application processes information needed to perform your playback choices, such as the selected track, playback position, and current playback state.

Where available features retain playlists, favourites, recent selections, or player preferences, those records are stored locally in the configuration described here. They are not transmitted to us as a remote listening history or used by us to build an advertising profile.

Support communications

If you email us, we receive your email address, the contents of your message, attachments you choose to include, and ordinary email delivery information. You may voluntarily provide a device model, Android version, app version, or description of a problem.

Please avoid sending passwords, identity documents, payment information, private recordings, or other sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request.

Accounts and automatic reporting

The configuration covered by this policy does not require an app account and does not send us automatic analytics, advertising identifiers, or SDK-generated crash reports. It does not require you to provide a name, email address, or telephone number for local music playback.

3. Permissions and Playback Controls

Music and audio access

Depending on the Android version and the method used to open music, Android may provide access through a music-and-audio permission, an older storage permission, or a grant for files you select. The Application uses the access made available to it for its music functions.

Declining or withdrawing access can prevent the Application from finding or playing affected files. Permission to access media is not permission to use that media for unrelated purposes.

Background and lock-screen playback

Where background playback is available and you start it, playback may continue while another app is open or your screen is locked.

Android media controls may display information about the current track, such as its title, artist, artwork, and playback state. Depending on your device and connections, this information may also be visible through compatible connected playback controls. Consider your lock-screen and connected-device settings when playing private audio.

Use the available playback controls to pause or stop playback. Closing the player screen is not necessarily the same as stopping background playback.

Microphone and unrelated information

Audio playback does not itself require recording your surroundings. The configuration covered here does not record microphone audio, telephone calls, or conversations. It does not access contacts, messages, call history, or precise location to provide local music playback.

You can review available permissions in your Android device settings. The names and locations of controls vary by device and Android version.

Official guidance: Android app permissions , shared media access , and background media playback .

4. How Information Is Used

Information processed locally is used to open and play selected audio, display relevant music information, operate playback controls, and retain supported preferences.

Information you separately send to us is used to respond to your communication, investigate the issue you report, handle privacy requests, and meet applicable legal obligations. Where necessary, it may also be used to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We do not use support correspondence to add you to marketing campaigns or build advertising profiles.

Legal grounds where applicable

Where a legal basis is required, processing support correspondence is based on our legitimate interest in responding to requests and resolving reported problems, subject to your rights and interests. Processing necessary to satisfy an applicable legal obligation is based on that obligation.

Where processing requires consent, consent must be obtained for the relevant activity. You may withdraw consent without affecting processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal. Installing the Application or continuing to use it is not a substitute for affirmative consent where the law requires it.

5. Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

The configuration described in this policy does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not use advertising identifiers, tracking pixels, or cross-app tracking technologies.

No online streaming, automatic remote artwork or lyrics lookup, or app-controlled cloud synchronisation is covered by this configuration. A version using those services would require disclosures identifying the relevant information, recipients, and purposes.

This HTML document contains no scripts, tracking pixels, externally loaded fonts, or embedded third-party content. The server used to host it may separately process connection information, such as an IP address and browser request details. The absence of tracking code in this document does not establish that its hosting service keeps no logs.

6. Information Sharing and External Services

We do not receive local music files or listening history through the described configuration and therefore do not disclose those records from our servers. We do not sell personal information received through support communications or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Information you separately provide to us may be disclosed in the following circumstances:

  • Business communication services: Email delivery, storage, and support-administration providers may process information necessary to handle your communication.
  • Legal requirements: Information may be disclosed where necessary to comply with a binding legal obligation or valid legal process.
  • Rights and safety: Information may be disclosed where legally permitted and necessary to protect people, investigate suspected unlawful activity, or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
  • Business changes: Relevant records may be involved in a merger, acquisition, reorganisation, or transfer of business assets, subject to applicable privacy requirements and any required notice or consent.
  • Your instructions: Information may be disclosed where you specifically request or lawfully authorise the disclosure.

Android, your device manufacturer, your app store, and services you independently use can process information under their own privacy notices. Their activities may include installations, updates, operating-system diagnostics, backups, or email delivery.

These independent activities are distinct from an SDK integrated into the Application. An integrated SDK's data practices remain part of the Application's disclosure responsibilities.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Music files

Music held in your device's shared media storage remains subject to your control and the controls of any service you use to store or back it up. Uninstalling a music player does not generally delete music files held in shared storage.

Local app records

Saved app preferences and supported local library records remain until they are replaced, reset, or removed. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the Application removes app-specific data according to Android's storage behaviour. Copies held in backups may have a separate lifecycle.

Support and privacy correspondence

Our retention policy is to keep support correspondence while resolving the request and any necessary follow-up. Afterwards, identifiable information is retained only where needed for an unresolved matter, an applicable legal obligation, or the establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims.

We may retain a limited record of a privacy request and our response where necessary to demonstrate appropriate handling. This does not justify keeping unrelated information that should otherwise be deleted.

When information is no longer needed for those purposes, our policy is to delete it or irreversibly anonymise it rather than retain identifiable records indefinitely.

Device backups

Depending on app configuration, Android version, and device settings, operating-system backup or device-transfer services may preserve or restore local app data. Manage those copies through the relevant device or backup-account controls.

Platform information: Android app-data backup .

8. Your Choices and Data Deletion

Control media access

Review the Application's permissions through Android settings. Revoking a permission limits future access covered by that permission; it does not necessarily erase information already stored.

Remove local app information

Use Android's app-storage controls to clear local app data, or uninstall the Application. Clearing app data can remove saved preferences and locally maintained playlists or library records, so preserve anything you need before taking that action.

To delete an original music file, use your device's file-management or media-storage controls. Removing a track from a playlist is not necessarily the same as deleting its underlying audio file.

Request deletion of information held by us

Email rajesh@monovatech.com with the subject Music player play your music — Data Deletion Request . Describe the correspondence or information concerned. Where possible, use the email address associated with the original communication.

We may request limited information where necessary and legally appropriate to verify the request. We cannot remotely retrieve or erase music and local player records that are stored only on your device.

Uninstalling the Application does not delete messages you previously sent to us or automatically submit a deletion request. If a lawful retention exception applies, we will explain the relevant reason to the extent permitted by law.

The described configuration has no app accounts, so there is no app account to close. This does not affect your ability to request deletion of personal information separately held by us.
