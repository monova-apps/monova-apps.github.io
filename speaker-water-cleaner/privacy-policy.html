2. Sound Playback and Device Access

In the configuration covered by this policy, the Application plays sound through your device's audio output. The playback function runs on the device and does not send audio to us or to a remote audio-processing service.

Sound playback is different from microphone recording. The covered configuration does not record speech, conversations, calls, or surrounding sounds. It does not create voice profiles or upload microphone recordings.

Playback choices and relevant audio settings are used to perform the sound function you request. Where the Application saves a preference for later use, that preference is kept in its local app storage, subject to any applicable operating system backup or device-transfer settings.

The covered configuration does not use access to your contacts, messages, call history, camera, personal media library, or precise location. It does not use accessibility access to read the contents of other apps.

You can review permissions made available for the Application through your Android device settings. Menu names and available controls vary by device and Android version.

Device guidance: Manage Android app permissions.
