Panorama 360 Camera is built to work entirely on your device.

Short version: no account, no upload. Your photos stay in your gallery.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

1. Camera

Camera frames are used only to compose the live preview and the final image. During a panorama or 360° sweep, frames are projected into an in-memory sphere and the result is written only when you finish the capture. Cancelling a sweep releases the frames. No camera frame is transmitted off the device.

2. Photos and the local gallery

Finished captures are saved to your device's public Pictures/Panorama 360 collection through Android's MediaStore. The app keeps a small local index of file URIs and metadata (capture type, resolution, date, favourite flag) in Android DataStore so the gallery opens instantly. This index is stored on the device, inside the app's private data directory, and is not shared.

When you choose Restore from device, Panorama 360 Camera asks for photo-library access and reads only the images inside Pictures/Panorama 360 to rebuild that local index. It does not upload or scan anything else. On Android 14+ you can grant access to selected photos only.

3. Location (optional, off by default)

If you enable location tagging, Panorama 360 Camera requests approximate location and writes the coordinates into the photo's EXIF metadata. Location is never transmitted anywhere. You can turn the setting off at any time in Settings, and existing photos keep the metadata they were saved with.

4. Permissions

Permission Why it is used
Camera Required to take photos, panoramas and spheres.
Read photos Requested only for Restore from device, to rebuild the local gallery index.
Approximate location Requested only when you enable location tagging.
Vibration Used for subtle shutter and progress haptics.
Write external storage Only on Android 8–9, where MediaStore saving required it.

Panorama 360 Camera does not request background location, contacts, microphone or notifications.

5. Collection and sharing

When you tap Share, the selected image is handed directly to the app you choose through Android's share sheet; that app's own privacy policy then applies.

6. Your control

You can delete captures from the gallery at any time. Deleting a capture removes the file from your device and removes it from Panorama 360 Camera's local index. Clearing app data removes the local index; your photos remain in Pictures/Panorama 360 and can be restored with the Restore action.

7. Children

Panorama 360 Camera is a general-purpose photography app intended for a general audience. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date above will change and the new version will be included in the app. Please review the updated policy when changes are published.
