1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to 100x Camera zoom (the “Application”), operated by Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. (“we,” “us,” “our,” or the “Service Provider”).

It explains how information is accessed, processed, stored, and disclosed when you use the Application in the configuration described above or communicate with us. It distinguishes information processed locally on your device from information received by the Service Provider and information that may be processed by advertising service providers.

Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. is responsible for personal information we receive and determine how to use, including support correspondence and privacy requests. Where applicable law uses the term “data controller,” we act as controller for that processing.

2. Information We Handle

Camera preview and captured photographs

The Application accesses the camera to display a live preview and capture photographs when you use its camera features. Camera frames are processed to provide the requested preview, framing, zoom controls, and image capture.

In the configuration covered by this policy, camera processing takes place on your device. We do not receive a remote camera feed or automatically upload your photographs to our servers.

Photographs and camera previews can contain personal information, including identifiable people, documents, addresses, and other private details. Local processing does not make that information anonymous.

Saved images and image metadata

When you save a photograph, the Application creates an image file in the storage location used by the relevant save function. Associated information can include a file name, capture date and time, image dimensions, and camera settings.

Where an image-opening feature is available, the Application accesses the images you make available to that feature. Existing files may contain additional metadata, including location information previously added by another app or device. This policy does not promise that metadata is automatically removed from every saved or shared image.

Settings and temporary information

The Application uses settings needed to carry out your camera choices. Where preferences or thumbnails are retained, the covered configuration stores them locally. Temporary processing information may also be used while preparing a preview or saving a photograph.

Advertising-related information

The Application displays advertisements. Advertising providers may process certain information about your device, application usage, advertising interactions, or other technical information as necessary to provide, measure, personalise, or improve advertising, subject to the provider's own privacy practices and applicable law.

Depending on the advertising configuration and your device or privacy settings, information processed for advertising may include identifiers such as an advertising ID, IP address, device or application information, approximate location, and information about interactions with advertisements. The Application does not use photographs from the camera as advertising material or provide your personal photographs to advertisers for advertising purposes.

Support and privacy communications

When you contact us, we receive your email address, message content, attachments you choose to send, and ordinary email delivery information. You may voluntarily include a device model, Android version, app version, screenshot, or description of a problem.

Please avoid sending private photographs, identity documents, passwords, or other sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request. Remove unrelated personal details from screenshots or sample images.

3. Camera and Photo Permissions

Camera access

Camera access is used to display the preview and capture photographs. Declining or withdrawing this permission prevents the camera functions that depend on it from operating.

Access to the camera does not, by itself, authorise uploading photographs, recording microphone audio, or using images for unrelated purposes.

Photo and storage access

The access needed to open or save images depends on the Android version, storage location, and file-access method. Access may involve a system picker, selected images, or a media or storage permission where required by the implementation.

Saving an app-created image and browsing an entire existing photo library are different activities. The configuration covered here limits image access to the camera functions and files you choose to use.

Other sensitive access

The described configuration does not record microphone audio, request precise location, access contacts or messages, or create biometric identifiers from faces. It does not send photographs to a facial-recognition service or use them to train an AI model.

You can review and change available app permissions through Android settings. Controls and menu names vary by device and Android version. Revoking access limits future access covered by that permission; it does not necessarily delete files already created.

Official guidance: Android app permissions and Android photo picker .

Information processed on your device is used to provide the camera preview, respond to your camera controls, capture and save photographs, open supported images, and retain available preferences.

Advertising-related information may be processed to display advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud or abuse, provide relevant advertising where permitted, and support the operation and improvement of advertising services.

We do not use support correspondence to enrol you in marketing campaigns or build advertising profiles. Advertising displayed within the Application may, however, be provided by independent advertising providers under their own privacy practices.

Legal grounds where required

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, responding to ordinary support correspondence is based on our legitimate interest in assisting users and resolving reported problems, subject to their rights and interests. Processing necessary to comply with an applicable legal obligation is based on that obligation.

Where an activity requires consent, consent must relate to that activity. You may withdraw consent without affecting processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal. Installing the Application, granting a device permission, or continuing to use the Application does not automatically provide consent for unrelated processing.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Photographs

Photographs saved to your device's shared media storage remain there until you or another authorised app or service removes them. Uninstalling the Application does not necessarily delete photographs saved outside its app-specific storage.

Your gallery or storage service may independently synchronise photographs to a cloud account. Such synchronisation is different from an upload initiated by the covered Application.

Local settings and temporary files

Local preferences and app-specific records remain until replaced, reset, or removed. Cached images and temporary files may remain until app cleanup, operating-system storage management, or your use of the device's cache and app-data controls removes them.

This policy does not promise that every temporary file is deleted immediately after capture. Clearing app data can remove app-specific information and files that have not been saved elsewhere.

Support and privacy-request records

Our retention policy is to keep support correspondence while resolving the request and necessary follow-up. Afterwards, identifiable information is retained only where necessary for an unresolved matter, an applicable legal obligation, or the establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims.

We may retain a limited record of a privacy request and our response where necessary to demonstrate appropriate handling. This does not justify retaining unrelated information that should otherwise be deleted.

Information no longer needed for a legitimate retention purpose is to be deleted or irreversibly anonymised.

Backups

Depending on app configuration, Android version, and device settings, backup or device-transfer services may preserve or restore local app data. Copies held in backups can have a separate lifecycle from data in the currently installed app.

Platform information: Android app-data backup .
