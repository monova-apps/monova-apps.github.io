1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to 100x Camera zoom (the “Application”), operated by Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. (“we,” “us,” “our,” or the “Service Provider”).

It explains how information is accessed, processed, stored, and disclosed when you use the Application, view or interact with advertisements, or communicate with us. It distinguishes information processed locally on your device from information received by us and information processed by advertising services.

The Application displays advertisements to support its operation and maintenance. Local camera processing does not mean that the Application has no network activity or advertising-related data collection.

Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. is responsible for personal information we receive and determine how to use, including support correspondence and privacy requests. Where applicable law uses the term “data controller,” we act as controller for that processing.

2. Information We Handle

Camera preview and captured photographs

The Application accesses the camera to display a live preview and capture photographs when you use its camera features. Camera frames are processed to provide the requested preview, framing, zoom controls, and image capture.

In the camera configuration described here, processing takes place on your device. We do not receive a remote camera feed or automatically upload your photographs to our servers.

Photographs and camera previews can contain personal information, including identifiable people, documents, addresses, and other private details. Local processing does not make that information anonymous.

Saved images and image metadata

When you save a photograph, the Application creates an image file in the storage location used by the relevant save function. Associated information can include a file name, capture date and time, image dimensions, and camera settings.

Where an image-opening feature is available, the Application accesses images you make available to that feature. Existing files may contain additional metadata, including location information previously added by another app or device. This policy does not promise that metadata is automatically removed from every saved or shared image.

Settings and temporary information

The Application uses settings needed to carry out your camera choices. Where preferences or thumbnails are retained, the described configuration stores them locally. Temporary processing information may also be used while preparing a preview or saving a photograph.

Advertising-related information

Advertising technology integrated into the Application may automatically collect or receive the following categories, depending on the provider, SDK configuration, device settings, and applicable consent:

  • Identifiers: an Android advertising identifier or other app or device identifiers supported by the advertising service.
  • Device and application information: device model, operating-system version, app version, language, and relevant technical settings.
  • Network information: IP address and connection information. An IP address may be used to estimate general location without accessing precise GPS location.
  • Advertising and interaction information: ad requests, impressions, clicks, video views, and app interactions measured by the advertising SDK.
  • Diagnostics: advertising SDK errors, crash information, performance measurements, and related technical data.
  • Privacy preferences: consent status and signals used to apply advertising choices and legal restrictions.

These categories describe potential advertising processing; they do not establish that every listed item is collected by every provider. Advertising information may be transmitted to provider servers separately from local camera processing.

Under the camera configuration described here, the Application does not provide advertising services with camera previews, captured photographs, selected images, or their contents for advertising purposes.

Support and privacy communications

When you contact us, we receive your email address, message content, attachments you choose to send, and ordinary email delivery information. You may voluntarily include a device model, Android version, app version, screenshot, or description of a problem.

Please avoid sending private photographs, identity documents, passwords, or sensitive information unnecessary for your request. Remove unrelated personal details from screenshots or sample images.

3. Camera and Other Permissions

Camera access

Camera access is used to display the preview and capture photographs. Declining or withdrawing this permission prevents camera functions that depend on it from operating.

Camera permission does not, by itself, authorise uploading photographs, recording microphone audio, or using images for unrelated purposes.

Photo and storage access

Access needed to open or save images depends on the Android version, storage location, and file-access method. Access may involve a system picker, selected images, or a media or storage permission where required by the implementation.

Saving an app-created image and browsing an entire existing photo library are different activities. The configuration described here limits image access to camera functions and files you choose to use.

Advertising and network access

Advertising services use network access to request and display advertisements. Depending on the integrated SDK and Android version, the Application may declare access relating to network status or the advertising identifier.

Some permissions are granted automatically rather than through a runtime prompt. General location estimated from an IP address is different from precise-location access.

Other sensitive access

The described camera configuration does not record microphone audio, request precise location, access contacts or messages, or create biometric identifiers from faces. It does not send photographs to a facial-recognition service or use them to train an AI model.

You can review and change available app permissions through Android settings. Controls vary by device and Android version. Revoking access limits future access covered by that permission; it does not necessarily delete existing files or information already transmitted.

Device permissions do not replace separate consent required for advertising or other personal-information processing.

Official guidance: Android app permissions and Android photo picker.
