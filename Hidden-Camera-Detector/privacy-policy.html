Privacy Policy

Hidden Camera Detector

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Hidden Camera Detector, provided by Monova Tech, handles information when you use the app. The app is designed to help users check their surroundings for possible hidden cameras using its available detection features.

Camera Access

If camera access is required by a detection feature, the app uses your device's camera to provide that functionality. Camera data is used for the feature you activate and is not intentionally uploaded to Monova Tech servers.

Device Sensors

The app may use supported device sensors, such as the magnetic field sensor, if available and required for its detection features. Sensor readings are used to provide information within the app and are not intentionally linked to your personal identity. Detection results are estimates and may not always be accurate.

Information Collection

The app does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location.

Data Storage

App settings, preferences, and detection history may be stored locally on your device if these features are available. The app does not intentionally upload this information to Monova Tech servers.

Data Sharing

Monova Tech does not intentionally sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties through this app. Information processed for the app's core detection features is not intentionally transmitted to Monova Tech servers.

Detection Limitations

Hidden Camera Detector provides tools that may help identify potential indicators of hidden cameras. It cannot guarantee detection of every hidden camera or surveillance device. Results may vary depending on your device, surroundings, sensors, and environmental conditions. Do not rely on the app as a guarantee that an area is free from surveillance.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. You are also responsible for maintaining the security of your device and any information stored on it.

Children's Privacy

The app does not intentionally collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published with a revised effective date.

Your Consent

By using Hidden Camera Detector, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
