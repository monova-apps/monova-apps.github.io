1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to Time Warp Scan: Face Filter (the “Application”), operated by Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd., Birtamod Municipality-4, Jhapa, Nepal (“we”, “us”, “our”, or the “Service Provider”).

The Application provides camera-based time-warp scan effects for creating distorted or stylised images and, where supported, video recordings. A scanning effect progressively captures portions of the camera view to create the result you request.

This policy distinguishes media processed locally on your device from information transmitted through advertising services and information you separately provide to us.

Features, controls, and permission requirements can vary by app release, device capabilities, and Android version. References to optional features apply when those features are included and used.

We are responsible for personal information whose purposes and means of processing we determine, including support correspondence and privacy requests. Where applicable law uses the term “controller”, we act as controller for that processing.

2. Information We Handle

Camera previews and scan effects

When you activate camera features, the Application accesses camera frames to display a live preview and create the selected time-warp effect. Processing may involve retaining portions of successive frames, composing an output image, displaying scan progress, and preparing a saved result.

For the local scan workflow described here, camera frames and effect processing remain on your device. They are not automatically uploaded to our servers or supplied to Google AdMob as advertising content. We do not receive a remote live camera feed.

Camera content may include faces, bodies, voices where audio is enabled, private surroundings, documents, and other identifiable information. A visual distortion does not guarantee that a person becomes unidentifiable.

Face images and biometric information

A time-warp effect can operate on image pixels without identifying the person in the image. In the scan workflow described here, faces are processed as visual content used to create the effect.

We do not use this workflow to identify or authenticate people, create facial identity templates, compare faces against an identity database, or build biometric profiles. We do not sell face images, provide them to advertisers, or use them to train artificial-intelligence models.

This policy does not authorise additional facial recognition, face-landmark collection, or biometric processing beyond the workflow described here. Any materially different feature requires appropriate disclosures and consent where applicable.

Saved images and recordings

When you save a result, the Application creates an image or other supported media file in the storage location used by that feature. If video capture or recording of the effect is included, camera frames are processed to create the recording you initiate.

Review saved results before sharing, particularly when they include other people or private background details.

Audio, where supported

A still-image scan does not require microphone audio. If your installed release supports sound recording, microphone access is used when you enable that feature and grant the necessary permission. Recorded sound may include voices and surrounding audio.

Selected media, metadata, and local settings

If an import or gallery feature is available, the Application accesses files you select or authorise it to access for the requested operation. Existing files may contain metadata added by another app or device.

Saved files may contain names, creation dates, dimensions, orientation, duration, and other technical metadata. Imported files may also contain location information. Saving or sharing does not guarantee removal of all metadata.

Available preferences, such as scan direction, speed, camera selection, or output settings, may be stored locally. Temporary frame buffers, thumbnails, and cached previews may be used to create and display results.

Advertising-related information

The Application uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Depending on the SDK version, configuration, consent choices, device settings, and applicable restrictions, advertising processing can include:

  • Network information: IP address and connection details. An IP address may be used to estimate general location.
  • Identifiers: Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other supported device or account-related identifiers where applicable and permitted.
  • Device and app information: device model, operating-system version, app version, language, and relevant technical settings.
  • Interactions: ad requests, impressions, clicks, video views, app launches, taps, and interactions measured by the SDK.
  • Diagnostics: errors, crash information, performance measurements, and related SDK technical data.
  • Privacy preferences: consent status and signals used to apply advertising choices and regional restrictions.

Advertising information may be transmitted to Google and relevant advertising service partners independently of local camera processing. Local effect processing does not mean the Application has no network activity or advertising-related data collection.

Support and privacy communications

When you contact us, we receive your email address, message content, voluntarily supplied attachments, and ordinary email delivery information. You may include your app version, device model, Android version, or a description of an issue.

Avoid sending sensitive images, private recordings, passwords, identity documents, or unrelated personal information. If you provide an example, remove private background details and information about other people unless necessary and appropriately authorised.

3. Device Permissions

Camera

Camera permission is used to display the preview, capture scan frames, and create the selected effect. Declining or revoking permission prevents camera features that depend on it from operating.

Camera permission does not, by itself, authorise uploading images, microphone recording, facial identification, or unrelated use of camera content.

Photos, videos, and storage

Access needed to open or save media depends on Android version, storage location, and implementation. The Application may use a system picker, shared media storage, or a media or storage permission where required.

A system picker can grant access to selected files without granting access to your entire library. Saving an app-created image and browsing existing media are different operations.

Microphone, where applicable

If an audio-recording feature is included, microphone permission is used for sound capture you activate. It is not required solely to create a silent still-image scan.

Network and advertising access

Internet access, network-state information, and advertising identifier access may be used for AdMob delivery, measurement, security, and related permitted processing. Some permissions do not produce a runtime prompt.

Other sensitive access and permission controls

The scan workflow described here does not require contacts, SMS, call history, or precise GPS location. General location inferred from an IP address is separate from GPS access.

You can review and change available permissions in Android Settings. Revoking permission limits future access covered by it but does not necessarily delete saved files or information already transmitted.

Device permissions do not replace separate advertising consent or other disclosures required by applicable law.
