3. Permissions and Wallpaper Display

Selecting photographs

On Android, a system photo picker can grant access to selected images without granting access to an entire photo library. The actual selection method must be identified for this Application.

A picker can offer images from a cloud-backed provider. Retrieving a selected image through that provider is different from the Application uploading it to another service.

Applying a wallpaper

Where direct wallpaper application is supported, the selected image is provided to the device's wallpaper system for the chosen destination. Android distinguishes home-screen and lock-screen wallpapers, subject to device support.

A wallpaper may be visible to anyone looking at your screen. A lock-screen image can reveal its contents before the device is unlocked. Consider whether you want names, photographs, or dates to be visible in that setting.

Changing the wallpaper, deleting an exported file, and deleting a project are separate actions. Removing a project should not be treated as confirmation that a previously applied image is no longer displayed.

Other device access

Capturing a new photograph is different from importing an existing one. Wallpaper customisation does not, by itself, establish a need to read contacts, messages, calls, or precise location.
