1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to HD Camera for Android 2026 (the “Application”), operated by Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd., Birtamod Municipality-4, Jhapa, Nepal (“we”, “us”, “our”, or the “Service Provider”).

The Application provides camera functionality for capturing photographs and, where included in your installed release, recording videos and using related media features.

This policy explains how information is accessed, processed, stored, and disclosed when you use the Application, interact with advertisements, or contact us. It distinguishes on-device media processing from advertising information transmitted to service providers and information you separately send to us.

Available features and permission requirements can vary by app release, device capabilities, and Android version. References to optional features apply when those features are included and used.

Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. is responsible for personal information whose purposes and means of processing we determine, including support correspondence and privacy requests. Where applicable law uses the term “controller”, we act as controller for that processing.

2. Information We Handle

Camera previews and photographs

When you activate the camera, the Application accesses camera frames to display a live preview, respond to available camera controls, and capture photographs when you request them.

The local camera workflow described here processes previews and captured media on your device. It does not automatically upload your photographs or live camera feed to our servers or provide their contents to AdMob for advertising.

Photographs and camera previews can contain personal information, including identifiable people, documents, addresses, and private surroundings. Processing that information locally does not make it anonymous.

Video and audio, where supported

If you use video recording, the Application processes camera frames to create the recording you request. If sound recording is available and enabled, it also accesses microphone audio after the necessary permission is granted.

Taking a still photograph does not, by itself, require microphone recording. Denying microphone permission may prevent sound recording or limit video features that depend on audio.

Selected media and editing

If your installed release includes gallery, import, preview, or editing features, the Application accesses media you select or authorise it to access for those operations. This can involve displaying an image, applying an available adjustment, or saving an output file.

Access granted through a system picker can be limited to selected files. The scope of any broader media permission depends on Android and the permission you grant.

Media metadata, settings, and temporary files

Saved media may contain file names, capture dates, dimensions, orientation, camera settings, video duration, and other technical metadata. Imported files may contain information previously added by another app or device, including location metadata.

The Application may retain local camera preferences, thumbnails, cached previews, and temporary files needed to process or save media. Exporting or sharing a file does not guarantee that all metadata is removed.

Advertising-related information

The Application uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Depending on the SDK version, configuration, device settings, consent choices, and applicable restrictions, advertising processing can include:

  • Network information: IP address and connection information. An IP address may be used to estimate general location.
  • Identifiers: Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other supported device or account-related identifiers where applicable and permitted.
  • Device and app information: device model, operating-system version, app version, language, and relevant technical settings.
  • Interactions: ad requests, impressions, clicks, video views, app launches, taps, and interactions measured by the SDK.
  • Diagnostics: SDK errors, crash information, performance measurements, and related technical data.
  • Privacy preferences: consent status and signals used to apply advertising choices and regional restrictions.

Advertising information may be transmitted to Google and relevant advertising service partners. Local camera processing does not mean the Application has no network activity or advertising-related data collection.

Support and privacy communications

When you contact us, we receive your email address, message content, voluntarily supplied attachments, and ordinary email delivery information. You may choose to include your device model, Android version, app version, or details of a technical issue.

Please avoid sending private photographs, recordings, identity documents, passwords, or unrelated sensitive information unless necessary for your request.

3. Device Permissions

Camera access

Camera permission is used to show the live preview, take photographs, and record video where supported. Declining or revoking permission prevents functions that require camera access.

Camera access does not, by itself, authorise uploading media, recording microphone audio, or using images for unrelated purposes.

Microphone access, where applicable

Microphone permission is used for sound in video recordings or another audio feature you activate, where included. It is not needed solely to capture a silent still image.

Photos, videos, and storage

Access needed to open or save media depends on Android version, storage location, and implementation. The Application may use a system picker, shared media storage, or a media or storage permission where required.

Saving a newly captured photograph and browsing existing media are different operations. Some save operations do not require broad access to your existing library.

Notifications and ongoing operations, where applicable

If an included feature uses notifications or a foreground service, these may display the status of an operation you initiate, such as an ongoing recording. Camera and microphone use remain subject to Android restrictions and applicable permissions.

Network and advertising access

Internet access, network-state information, and advertising identifier access may be used for ad delivery, measurement, security, and other permitted advertising processing. Some permissions do not produce a runtime prompt.

Managing access

You can review and change available permissions in Android Settings. Revoking a permission limits future access covered by it but does not necessarily delete existing files or information already transmitted.

The camera workflow described in this policy does not require contacts, SMS, call history, or precise GPS location. Approximate location inferred from an IP address is separate from GPS access.

Device permissions do not replace separate advertising consent or other disclosures required by applicable law.

Settings and temporary files

Local preferences, thumbnails, previews, and temporary files may remain until replaced, cleaned up, cleared through Android storage controls, or removed during uninstall. Interrupted operations may leave incomplete or temporary files.

Clearing app data can permanently remove app-specific information and files not saved elsewhere. Android backup or device-transfer settings may preserve or restore some data.

Support and privacy-request records

We retain identifiable correspondence while resolving your request and necessary follow-up. Afterwards, information is retained only where necessary for an unresolved matter, legal obligation, or legal claim.

Limited records of privacy requests and our responses may be retained to demonstrate appropriate handling. Information no longer needed is deleted or irreversibly anonymised.

Advertising information

Google and other relevant advertising providers retain information according to their policies, purposes, and legal requirements. Retention can differ by information category and purpose.

Clearing app data, deleting an advertising identifier, or uninstalling the Application does not necessarily erase information already received by those providers.
