1. About this policy

This policy explains how the operator of Ragya - Track Your Rides ("Ragya", "we", "our" or "us") handles personal information through the Android app, its connected backend, operator console, support service and related account-management webpages.

Ragya helps passengers and drivers coordinate local journeys, including the Banyani–Birtamode corridor in Jhapa, Nepal. It provides live vehicle positions, seat information, pickup requests and published stand departure times. It is not an emergency service.

This policy describes the current service. It does not treat installing or using the app as blanket consent to every possible use of your information. We request relevant device permissions and provide additional disclosures or obtain consent when required.

2. Information we collect

Account and contact information

When you register as a passenger or driver, we collect your name, phone number, account role and password, and assign an account identifier. Our server stores a salted password hash rather than your readable password. Authentication credentials travel over an encrypted connection. We also process session credentials, account status and access or approval information.

Driver and vehicle information

Driver registration includes vehicle type and model, vehicle registration or plate number, seating capacity, driving-licence number and a licence photograph. These are used for operator review, driver access and ride operation. Drivers may also provide departure schedules and seat-occupancy updates. Licence submission is not a guarantee of an independent government verification.

Trip and pickup information

We process vehicle GPS coordinates and their timestamps, location accuracy, speed when supplied by the device, direction of travel, trip start and end information, vehicle availability, seat states and scheduled or actual departures.

For pickup requests, we process the passenger and driver identifiers, requested seats, chosen pickup coordinates, landmark or pickup label, any note you enter, request timestamps and the request's status, such as pending, accepted, boarded, completed or cancelled.

Support messages

When you use in-app support, we store message contents, account association, sender type, timestamps and conversation status. Email support also involves your email address, message and any material you choose to send. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, medical information or unnecessary identity documents in support messages.

Operations, subscriptions and technical information

We maintain operator actions, account-access changes, subscription or trial grants, manual payment-ledger entries and service activity totals. Manual ledger entries can include amounts, dates, access periods and the record type. The current app does not collect payment-card numbers or process card payments.

Our infrastructure necessarily handles network information, including IP addresses, to receive requests and protect the service. Technical request information, error information and security records may be processed by the hosting infrastructure. We also maintain first-party aggregate counts for operating and understanding the service.

2a. Isolated practice accounts

Dedicated practice accounts use a separate database and are clearly marked as a practice environment. Their vehicles, counterparties and bookings are simulated, do not appear in the public ride feed, and do not arrange real transport. Practice accounts cannot access real users or public pickup records. Synthetic phone contacts cannot be called.

When a practice user starts GPS sharing, sends a pickup request or writes a support message, that information is transmitted to our server and processed in the isolated practice environment. Android permissions and map-provider network processing still apply. Do not submit real passengers' details, licence documents or other sensitive information through shared practice accounts.

Practice support responses are explicitly labelled automated. Deleting a practice account removes its associated account data and invalidates its sessions. The operator-provided synthetic identity can be recreated from a protected fixture on the next sign-in so the practice workflow remains available. This recreation applies only to designated practice identities; ordinary deleted user accounts are not automatically recreated.

3. Location and trip sharing

Drivers: public location during an active trip

When you start a trip and share GPS, your vehicle's location becomes visible to people viewing live rides. Your public ride information can include the vehicle plate, type or model, direction, available seats, the age of the latest GPS update and departure information. Published stand times and destinations are also public.

Active driver tracking uses an Android foreground location service with a visible notification. It can continue when you leave the app or turn off the screen. The current app does not request Android's separate background-location permission. Permission names do not change the fact that an explicitly started trip can continue sharing while the app is not on screen.

Use the end-trip control to stop trip sharing. You can also revoke location permission in Android settings. An internet connection is needed for the server to receive a stop request; if communication is interrupted, the last transmitted position may remain temporarily until it becomes stale. The live feed normally excludes GPS updates older than approximately 60 seconds.

Ending a trip stops live sharing; it does not immediately erase every related trip, pickup, support or audit record. Retention is explained below. Other people may have seen or recorded information while it was public, and we cannot recall their independent copies.

Passengers: your chosen pickup point

You can place a pickup pin manually, or permit location access to help find your current position. A pickup coordinate may reveal where you are or where you plan to wait. When you submit a request, the assigned driver and authorised operator can access the pickup details needed to coordinate it. Passenger locations and phone numbers are not included in the public vehicle feed.

The passenger app does not continuously publish your movements as a live vehicle. Merely viewing the map does not register you as a driver or start a driver trip.

4. Device permissions and local storage

  • Location: used for active driver GPS sharing and the passenger's optional current-location pickup feature. You can choose a pin manually without granting passenger location access. Driver tracking requires suitable location access.
  • Notifications: used for active-trip status and pickup updates. Disabling notifications may prevent you from seeing important updates; it is not a substitute for ending a trip.
  • Selected photographs: driver licence images are selected through the Android document picker. This does not give Ragya general permission to upload your entire photo library.
  • Internet and foreground services: required for maps, account access, live rides, active trip sharing and pickup-status communication.

Ragya stores preferences such as your language, map-related cached content and protected session information on your device. The app protects stored authentication tokens using Android Keystore-backed encryption. Android application backup is disabled in the current release.

The current app does not request access to your address book, SMS inbox, call logs or microphone. If you choose to call a driver or advertiser or open another map app, Android hands that action to the relevant external application. Ragya does not record those calls.

You can manage permissions, notifications and local storage in Android settings. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app does not, by itself, delete your server account.

5. How and why we use information

  • Create, authenticate and manage accounts.
  • Review driver applications and administer trial or subscription access.
  • Display active vehicles, seat availability and departure information.
  • Coordinate pickup requests, seat holds, boarding and trip completion.
  • Provide support and respond to privacy or deletion requests.
  • Maintain service reliability, detect misuse, prevent conflicting seat bookings and secure accounts.
  • Maintain necessary operational, audit and accounting records and comply with applicable obligations.
  • Display operator-managed advertisements and understand aggregate service activity.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on the basis appropriate to the activity: providing the service you request, your consent for optional processing where required, compliance with legal obligations, or legitimate operational and security interests where recognised by law and balanced against your rights. We do not assume that one legal basis or one jurisdiction's rules apply to every user or activity.

Refusing information essential to an account, driver review or pickup can prevent us from providing that particular feature. Withdrawing an optional permission does not make earlier lawful processing unlawful.

6. Who can see information

  • People viewing live rides: public vehicle position, vehicle details, seat availability, direction and published departure information.
  • The assigned passenger and driver: relevant pickup and contact details so they can identify the ride, communicate and meet. Driver contact details may be shown to the passenger involved in a pickup.
  • Authorised operator personnel: registration details, licence documents, account information, operational records, pickup details and support conversations as needed to run and support Ragya.
  • Service providers: hosting, maps, network delivery and email providers process information needed for the service they provide. Map requests are explained in section 8.
  • Authorities or professional advisers: information may be disclosed where necessary to comply with a lawful requirement, protect rights or safety, investigate misuse, or obtain professional advice, subject to applicable law.
  • A successor operator: if responsibility for the service changes through a reorganisation or transfer, relevant information may transfer subject to applicable protections and any required notice.

We do not sell personal information or provide passenger profiles, driver licence photos, private pickup pins or support-message contents to advertisers for advertising purposes. We do not publish licence photographs or support conversations in the public ride feed.

7. Advertisements and promotions

Ragya - Track Your Rides may contain advertisements, sponsored content and local business promotions. Advertising can help fund operation of the service. Availability of advertisements can vary by operator configuration and time.

The current release supports advertisements managed by the Ragya operator. It does not include Google AdMob or another advertising-network SDK, does not request the Android advertising ID, and does not implement advertising-network cross-app tracking. Local promotions are not described as personalised advertising based on your private pickup history or support messages.

If you choose to call an advertiser, open an external destination or give a business information directly, that business or external service will handle the resulting interaction under its own privacy practices. A displayed advertisement is not a guarantee or endorsement of the advertiser's services.

If a future release introduces an advertising SDK, personalised advertising, additional advertising measurement or materially different data sharing, we will update the relevant disclosures and obtain consent or provide controls where required before that processing begins. This statement does not authorise those future activities in the current release.

8. Maps and other services

Ragya uses the MapLibre map renderer and map data attributed to OpenStreetMap contributors. Styles, tiles and related map resources are loaded from OpenFreeMap. The map service and its delivery infrastructure necessarily receive connection information such as your IP address and requests identifying the map resources or areas being viewed. An IP address being received to deliver content does not necessarily mean it is retained in an access log.

Read the OpenFreeMap Privacy Policy for its current processing and retention practices. OpenStreetMap attribution and licensing information is available at OpenStreetMap Copyright and Licence.

If you open an external map, email, telephone, website or app-store service, that provider's policies also apply to the interaction. The Ragya privacy policy does not govern independently operated services.

This policy page contains no advertising or analytics scripts, external fonts or tracking pixels. Loading the page still requires normal network requests to its hosting infrastructure. Essential app and administrator session handling is used for authentication, not advertising tracking.

9. Retention and deletion periods

We keep information for the service and the purposes described above, subject to the following current arrangements. Automated cleanup runs periodically, so a retention boundary is not a guarantee of deletion at an exact second.

  • Account profiles and driver documents: retained while the account is maintained, unless replaced or deleted through an applicable process. Account deletion removes the profile and associated private licence data from the active service.
  • Live location: the public feed uses recent GPS updates rather than indefinitely displaying an old position. Ending a trip clears its active location-sharing state. Associated operational records can remain under the periods below.
  • Closed pickup details: normally removed approximately 24 hours after the latest update once the pickup is no longer pending, accepted or boarded.
  • In-app support messages: kept for up to 365 days, or removed earlier when the associated account is deleted. Resolved conversations are still subject to this retention period.
  • Journey and administration audit records: normally retained for 365 days. The operator can configure operational history retention; any material change affecting users will be reflected in the relevant privacy information.
  • Departure records: automatically cleaned up approximately 365 days after the scheduled time.
  • Aggregate daily activity counts: retained for up to 730 days. These totals do not contain the individual pickup-pin details.
  • Authentication sessions: user sessions normally expire after 30 days and administrator sessions after 8 hours, unless revoked sooner. Expired sessions are cleaned up.
  • Manual access and payment-ledger records: not currently subject to the same automatic expiry as pickup or chat records. Necessary records may remain after account deletion for accounting, dispute resolution or legal obligations. You may contact us about their retention or removal.
  • Encrypted service backups: retained on a rolling basis for up to 14 days. A separately held disaster-recovery copy may also remain for up to 14 days. Deleted information can remain in a restricted backup until that copy expires.

Email support correspondence and privacy-request records are handled separately from the in-app chat database and kept as necessary to resolve the request, document its handling and meet applicable obligations. Technical and security logs are limited according to operational need and provider settings; the in-app chat's 365-day limit should not be assumed to be the retention period for every provider log.

Where a specific legal obligation or dispute requires longer retention, we may retain the necessary records with restricted access for that purpose. We do not use that exception as permission to retain every category indefinitely.

10. Account deletion

You can request deletion using either of these authenticated self-service options:

  • In Ragya, open Account or your driver Profile, then choose Delete my account and confirm.
  • Use the Ragya web account-deletion page.

If you cannot access your account, email support@monovatech.com with the subject "Ragya account deletion". Include enough information to identify your account, such as the registered phone number, but do not send your password. We may need proportionate identity verification to protect you from an unauthorised deletion request.

Deletion removes the account profile, private licence information, associated support conversations and linked pickup records from the active service, ends active sharing and invalidates sessions. Relevant journey history is anonymised or disconnected from the deleted account. Restricted audit or manual accounting records can remain as described in section 9, and encrypted backup copies expire according to their retention schedule.

Uninstalling the app, signing out, ending a trip or marking a support conversation resolved does not itself delete the account. Deletion cannot remove independent records already held by a driver, passenger or external provider outside our control; their own obligations may apply.

12. Security

We use measures appropriate to the service, including HTTPS for app-to-server communication, salted password hashing, protected authentication tokens, account-based access controls, restricted administrative access and encrypted service backups. Private driver documents and support messages are not part of the public live-rides response.

No network, device or storage system is completely secure. Protect your device and login details, use a password you do not reuse elsewhere, and contact us promptly if you suspect unauthorised access. We will investigate security incidents and notify affected people or authorities where applicable law requires it.

13. International processing

Ragya serves users in Nepal, but hosting, mapping, network-delivery or email infrastructure may process information outside Nepal or your country of residence. Privacy laws in those locations may differ. Where applicable law requires safeguards or restrictions for a transfer, we will apply the required protections. We do not represent that every service provider or all stored data is located in Nepal.

14. Children and younger passengers

Ragya is a general local-transport service and is not designed to solicit personal information from young children. Driver accounts are intended for adults who hold the appropriate driving licence and meet the operator's requirements.

Where a younger passenger cannot lawfully consent or contract for a service, a parent or guardian should manage the journey and any necessary information sharing. Do not create an account or submit a child's personal information without any parental or guardian authorisation required by applicable law. Avoid including children's personal details in pickup notes or support messages unless necessary and appropriately authorised.

If you believe a child has provided personal information without the required authorisation, contact us. We will assess the report and take appropriate steps, including deletion where required.
