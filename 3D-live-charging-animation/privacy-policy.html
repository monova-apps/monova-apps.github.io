1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to 3D live charging animation (the “Application”), operated by Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. (“we,” “us,” “our,” or the “Service Provider”).

The Application's intended function is to display visual animations associated with your device's charging state. It contains advertisements that support its operation and maintenance.

This policy explains information handling when you use the Application, view or interact with advertisements, or send us a message. It distinguishes information processed locally from information transmitted to us or processed through advertising services.

Where applicable law uses the term “data controller,” we act as controller for personal information we receive and determine how to use, including support and privacy correspondence.

2. Information We Handle

Battery level and charging state

In the charging configuration described here, the Application reads information Android makes available about battery percentage and whether the device is charging, fully charged, connected to power, or disconnected. Relevant connection-type information may be used where needed by the charging feature.

These signals are used locally to start, update, or stop the visual display. Under this configuration, they are not transmitted to us or retained by us as a remote charging history.

Animation settings and interactions

The Application processes your animation selection and interactions with its controls. Saved preferences may include an animation choice, an enabled or disabled setting, and other display options offered by the installed version.

Display dimensions and orientation may be used locally to render animations correctly. This does not include recording the contents of your screen under the described configuration.

Advertising-related information

Advertising technology integrated into the Application may automatically collect or receive the following information, depending on the advertising provider, SDK configuration, device settings, and applicable consent:

  • Identifiers: an Android advertising identifier or other app or device identifiers supported by the advertising service.
  • Device and application information: device model, operating-system version, app version, language, and relevant technical settings.
  • Network information: IP address and connection information. An IP address may be used to estimate general location without accessing precise GPS location.
  • Advertising and interaction information: ad requests, impressions, clicks, video views, and interactions measured by the advertising SDK.
  • Diagnostic information: advertising SDK errors, crash information, performance measurements, and related technical data.
  • Privacy preferences: consent status and signals used to apply advertising choices and legal restrictions.

These categories describe potential advertising processing; they do not establish that every listed item is collected by every provider. Advertising information may be transmitted to provider servers separately from local charging-animation functionality.

Support and privacy communications

If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, attachments, and ordinary email delivery information. You may voluntarily include your device model, Android version, app version, or details about an issue.

Remove private notifications, account names, and unrelated information from screenshots before sending them. Do not send passwords, payment details, or unnecessary identity documents.

3. Charging Events and Background Operation

Android provides battery and power-connection information that apps can use to respond to charging changes. Where automatic animations are implemented and enabled, the Application uses relevant events to respond when power is connected or disconnected.

This may involve local operation while the main app screen is not open. Background availability depends on the implementation, Android version, and device restrictions.

Under the described charging configuration, this operation supports the selected display and its controls. It does not monitor the contents of other apps or create a developer-held charging-session history.

Advertising services may independently measure app or ad interactions as described in this policy. Local operation of the charging feature does not mean that the Application has no advertising-related network communication.

Leaving the main app screen may not disable an enabled automatic feature. Use available feature controls or Android app-info controls to stop the Application when needed.

4. Permissions and Display Access

Display over other apps

Where an overlay-based display is implemented, the Application may request Android's “Display over other apps” special access to show the chosen animation above another app when the relevant feature is enabled.

Android requires applicable user authorisation for this access on supported versions. You can revoke it in special app-access settings; the overlay feature then cannot operate.

Under the described configuration, the overlay displays graphics and handles its own controls. It is not used to capture the screen, read other apps' contents, collect passwords, or bypass device security.

Notifications

Where notifications are used for service status or controls, the Application displays its own notifications. This is different from notification-listener access and does not authorise reading other apps' messages or notifications.

Advertising and network access

Advertising services use network access to request and display advertisements. Depending on the integrated SDK and Android version, the Application may declare access relating to network status or the advertising identifier.

Some permissions are granted automatically rather than through a runtime prompt. General location estimated from an IP address differs from precise-location access.

Other sensitive capabilities

The described charging configuration does not use camera or microphone recording, contacts, messages, call history, precise location, broad photo-library access, or an accessibility service.

A version that imports personal media, downloads content through additional providers, or uses other sensitive capabilities requires disclosures reflecting those practices.

Permission controls and background restrictions vary by device. Granting permission for a display feature does not replace separate consent required for advertising or other personal-information processing.
