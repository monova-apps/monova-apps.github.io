Privacy Policy

Speaker Cleaner

This Privacy Policy explains how Monova Tech ("Service Provider") handles information when you use the Speaker Cleaner mobile application (the "Application").

Speaker Cleaner is designed to provide speaker-cleaning functionality by playing specially generated audio tones through your device's speaker.

Information Collection

Speaker Cleaner does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location.

The Application does not intentionally collect or transmit the audio played through your device's speaker to the Service Provider.

In short: Speaker Cleaner is designed to work directly on your device without requiring you to provide personal information.

Audio Functionality

The Application uses your device's speaker to play audio frequencies for its speaker cleaning functionality.

Audio is generated and played locally on your device. The Application does not upload, store, or transmit the generated audio to the Service Provider.

Speaker Cleaner does not require microphone access for its core functionality.

Device Permissions

The Application may use basic device functionality required to play audio and provide its features.

Speaker Cleaner does not require access to your contacts, camera, photos, location, or microphone for its core speaker-cleaning functionality.

Data Sharing

Speaker Cleaner does not intentionally sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Because the Application does not require an account or intentionally collect personal information, there is no personal profile created by the Application for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Storage and Retention

Speaker Cleaner does not intentionally store personal information on the Service Provider's servers.

Since the Application does not require an account or collect personal information for its core functionality, there is no personal account data to retain after you stop using the Application.

Children's Privacy

Speaker Cleaner does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to the Service Provider, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

Security

The Service Provider values your privacy and takes reasonable measures to protect information under its control.

Because Speaker Cleaner is designed to operate locally and does not intentionally collect personal information, the Application minimizes the amount of personal information that could be exposed.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by publishing an updated version of this Privacy Policy with a new effective date.

You are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

Your Consent

By using Speaker Cleaner, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Speaker Cleaner, please contact the Service Provider at monova.apps@proton.me .
