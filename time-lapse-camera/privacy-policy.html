1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to Time Lapse Camera: Video Maker (the “Application”), operated by Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd., Birtamod Municipality-4, Jhapa, Nepal (“we”, “us”, “our”, or the “Service Provider”).

The Application enables users to capture time-lapse sequences and create videos. This policy describes how information is accessed, processed, stored, and disclosed when you use its features, interact with advertisements, or contact us.

It distinguishes media processed locally on your device from information handled by advertising services and information you separately provide to us. Available features, controls, and permissions can vary by app release, device, and Android version.

We are responsible for personal information whose purposes and means of processing we determine, including support correspondence and privacy requests. Where applicable law uses the term “data controller”, we act as controller for that processing.

2. Information We Handle

Camera previews and time-lapse recordings

When you activate camera features, the Application accesses camera frames to display a preview and capture a sequence at your selected interval. These frames are used to create the recording or video you request.

The local time-lapse workflow described in this policy processes camera frames and video content on your device. It does not automatically transmit your live camera feed or recordings to our servers or supply their contents to AdMob for advertising.

Camera content may contain identifiable people, private surroundings, documents, or other personal information. Local processing does not make that content anonymous.

Selected media and video creation

If your installed release supports importing media, the Application accesses images, videos, or audio files that you select or authorise it to access. These files are used for the requested project, editing operation, preview, or export.

Video creation may involve assembling frames, adjusting playback speed, rendering previews, and encoding output files. Access to selected media does not, by itself, authorise unrelated use of the rest of your media library.

Audio, where supported

Capturing a silent time-lapse sequence does not require microphone audio. If your installed release offers microphone recording, audio is accessed when you activate that feature and grant the required permission. An imported audio track is accessed when you select it for a supported feature.

Files, metadata, settings, and temporary information

Media files and project records may include file names, creation dates, duration, dimensions, orientation, frame rate, capture intervals, and export settings. Existing files may contain metadata added by another app or device, including location information.

The Application may store local preferences, project information, thumbnails, temporary frames, and cached previews to support recording and export. Saving or sharing media does not guarantee that all metadata is removed.

Advertising-related information

Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK process advertising-related information. The specific information depends on the SDK version, configuration, device settings, consent choices, and applicable restrictions. It can include:

  • Network information: IP address and connection information. An IP address may be used to estimate general location.
  • Identifiers: Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other device or account-related identifiers supported by the SDK where applicable and permitted.
  • Device and application details: device model, operating-system version, app version, language, and relevant technical settings.
  • Interaction information: ad requests, impressions, clicks, video views, app launches, taps, and interactions measured by the SDK.
  • Diagnostics: errors, crash information, performance measurements, and SDK diagnostic data.
  • Privacy preferences: consent status and signals used to apply advertising choices and regional restrictions.

Advertising information may be transmitted to Google and relevant advertising service partners. On-device video processing does not mean that the Application has no network activity or advertising data collection.

Support and privacy communications

When you contact us, we receive your email address, message content, attachments you choose to send, and ordinary email delivery information. You may voluntarily provide your device model, Android version, app version, or a description of an issue.

Please avoid sending sensitive recordings, passwords, identity documents, or unrelated personal information unless necessary for a specific request.

3. Device Permissions

Camera

Camera permission is used to display the preview and capture frames for recordings you initiate. Declining or revoking this permission prevents camera features that depend on it from operating.

Photos, videos, and storage

Media access is used to open selected source files and save recordings, projects, or exported videos. Depending on Android version and implementation, this may use a system picker, shared media storage, or a media or storage permission. Some storage operations do not require a separate runtime permission.

Microphone, where applicable

If an audio-recording feature is included, microphone permission is used for audio capture you initiate. It is not needed solely to capture silent time-lapse frames.

Foreground services and notifications, where applicable

An ongoing recording or export may use a foreground service and status notification where supported. Continuing camera use while the interface is not visible depends on Android restrictions and the feature involved. Camera recording must be initiated by you.

Network and advertising access

Internet access, network-state information, and advertising identifier access may be used for AdMob delivery, measurement, and related permitted processing. Some permissions are granted automatically rather than through a runtime prompt.

Other sensitive access

The time-lapse workflow described here does not require contacts, SMS, call history, or precise GPS location. Approximate location inferred from an IP address is separate from GPS access.

You can manage available permissions through Android Settings. Revoking a permission limits future access covered by it but does not necessarily delete existing files or information already transmitted. Device permissions do not replace separate advertising consent where legally required.

Saved recordings and exports remain in their storage location until you or another authorised app or service removes them. Uninstalling the Application may leave files saved to shared storage or your gallery.

Copies in cloud accounts, backups, or recipients’ systems follow those services’ separate retention rules.

Projects, preferences, and temporary files

Local project information, preferences, and temporary files may remain until overwritten, cleaned up, cleared through Android storage controls, or removed during uninstall. Interrupted recordings or exports may leave temporary frames or incomplete files.

Clearing app data can permanently remove local projects and files not saved elsewhere. Device backup or transfer services may preserve or restore some information, depending on configuration.

Support and privacy records

We retain identifiable correspondence while resolving your request and necessary follow-up. Afterwards, information is retained only where needed for an unresolved matter, legal obligation, or legal claim.

A limited record of a privacy request and our response may be retained to demonstrate appropriate handling. Information no longer needed is deleted or irreversibly anonymised.

Advertising information

Google and relevant advertising providers retain information according to their policies, processing purposes, and legal requirements. Retention varies by information category and purpose.
