1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to Traffic Arrow Puzzle Escape (the “Game”), operated by Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. (“we,” “us,” “our,” or the “Service Provider”).

Traffic Arrow Puzzle Escape is a free offline puzzle game involving arrow-directed vehicles and a traffic escape challenge. This policy explains information handling for the local-only version described above and for communications you choose to send us.

Processing game information on your device is different from sending it to the Service Provider. We distinguish those activities throughout this policy.

Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. is responsible for personal information we receive and determine how to use, including support correspondence and privacy requests. Where applicable law uses the term “data controller,” we act as controller for that processing.

2. Gameplay Information

Information used to operate the puzzle

The local-only Game processes the information needed to display a puzzle and respond to your actions. This includes the current level, vehicle positions, selected moves, the number of cleared vehicles, the displayed coin balance, and information needed to carry out Hint, Undo, and Restart actions.

Your interactions with the Game's controls are used to update the puzzle. They are not sent to us as a remote tap history, gameplay recording, or behavioural profile in the version covered here.

Saved progress and preferences

Progress and preferences retained between sessions are stored in the Game's local app storage. These records allow the Game to retain supported progress and settings without a developer-operated online account.

The local-only version does not upload saved levels, coin balances, hint usage, or other game records to our servers. Device backup and device-transfer services may operate separately, as explained in Section 8.

Technical information processed locally

The Game uses the device capabilities needed to render graphics and respond to input. For example, display dimensions can be used to fit the puzzle and controls to the screen.

Under the covered configuration, these operations do not create a remote device profile or send automatic performance reports to us.

Game coins and payments

In the version covered by this policy, the displayed coin balance is maintained as part of local game state. This version does not process purchases, subscriptions, payment-card details, or billing records.

3. Information You Send to Us

When you email us, we receive the information included in that communication. This can include your email address, name if provided, message, attachments, and ordinary email delivery information.

You may voluntarily include a device model, operating-system version, Game version, level number, or description of a problem to help us investigate your request.

Screenshots may reveal notifications, account names, or other private information outside the Game. Remove unrelated details before sending them. Please do not send passwords, payment information, or unnecessary identity documents.

We use correspondence to respond to the issue you raise and handle any related follow-up. Support messages are not used to enrol you in marketing campaigns.

4. Device Access and Permissions

The local-only puzzle configuration does not use camera access, microphone recording, contacts, messages, call history, precise location, broad photo-library access, screen recording, or accessibility-service access.

The vehicles, roads, and surrounding scenery are part of the puzzle. They are not used to track your physical location or collect information about your real-world journeys.

Local game records are kept in app-specific storage. The covered configuration does not browse unrelated files or personal media to provide puzzle gameplay.

You can review permissions available for the installed Game through your device settings. The controls and menu names vary by operating system and device.

Granting a permission for a particular feature does not authorise unrelated processing. Revoking a permission limits future access covered by it but does not necessarily erase information already stored.

5. Advertising, Analytics, and External Services

The local-only Game

The version covered by this policy contains no advertising, analytics, or automatic crash-reporting integrations. It does not use advertising identifiers, cross-app tracking, online accounts, online leaderboards, or developer-operated cloud saving.

These statements apply to the described configuration. An SDK that transmits device or gameplay information would be part of the Game's data practices and would require accurate additional disclosures.

App stores and operating-system services

The app store, your operating system, and your device manufacturer may independently process information for installation, updates, platform diagnostics, device backups, or account security. Their activities are governed by the relevant services and your settings.

Platform-provided diagnostics are not the same as diagnostic collection built into the Game. Offline gameplay is not a statement that your entire device performs no network activity.

Support providers

Providers involved in email delivery, message storage, and support administration may process the information necessary to handle communications you send us.

This privacy-policy page

This HTML document contains no scripts, tracking pixels, advertising, external fonts, or embedded third-party media. Its hosting server may separately handle connection information, including an IP address and browser request details.

The absence of tracking code in this document does not establish that its hosting service keeps no access logs.

6. Purposes and Legal Grounds

Local gameplay information is used to operate the puzzle, carry out your selected actions, display progress, and retain supported settings. It is not received by us for advertising or remote profiling in the covered version.

Personal information you separately provide is used to answer communications, investigate reported issues, handle privacy requests, satisfy applicable legal obligations, and, where necessary and lawful, establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Where a legal basis is required, ordinary support correspondence is handled on the basis of our legitimate interest in assisting players, subject to their rights and interests. Processing required by law is based on the relevant legal obligation. Consent is relied on where the particular activity requires it.

You may withdraw consent for processing that depends on it. Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully carried out beforehand. Installing or playing the Game is not a substitute for affirmative consent where required.

7. When Information Is Disclosed

We do not receive local gameplay records through the covered configuration and therefore do not disclose those records from our servers.

Information you separately send us may be disclosed in the following circumstances:

  • Communication and support services: Where necessary for providers to deliver, store, or help us respond to your message.
  • Legal obligations: Where necessary to comply with a binding legal requirement or valid legal process.
  • Rights and safety: Where necessary and legally permitted to protect people, investigate unlawful activity, or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
  • Business changes: In connection with a merger, acquisition, reorganisation, or transfer of relevant business assets, subject to applicable privacy requirements.
  • Your instructions: Where you specifically request or lawfully authorise the disclosure.

Under this policy, personal information received through support communications is not sold or shared for cross-context behavioural advertising. Removing a person's name alone does not make a record anonymous.

8. Storage and Data Retention

Local progress and game settings

Saved progress and preferences remain in local app storage until replaced, reset, or removed. Temporary game resources may remain until app cleanup, operating-system storage management, or your use of storage controls removes them.

Restarting a puzzle is different from deleting all saved progress. An Undo action or a level restart should not be treated as a request to erase all locally stored Game information.

Support correspondence

Our retention policy is to keep identifiable correspondence while addressing the request and necessary follow-up. After that, further retention is limited to an unresolved dispute, an applicable legal obligation, or another specific lawful need.

Information that is no longer needed for those purposes is to be deleted or irreversibly anonymised rather than retained indefinitely.

Privacy-request records

We may retain a limited record of a privacy request and our response where necessary to demonstrate appropriate handling. This does not justify keeping unrelated information that should otherwise be deleted.

Device backups and transfers

Depending on the Game's backup configuration, operating-system version, and device settings, backup or device-transfer services may preserve or restore local progress and preferences. Backup copies can have a separate lifecycle from the currently installed Game.

These services are distinct from a cloud-save account operated by us. Manage relevant backups through the controls of the device or backup provider.

9. Your Choices and Data Deletion

Remove information from your device

Use your device's app-management controls to clear the Game's local storage or uninstall the Game. On Android, app-storage controls are generally available through the Game's app-info screen in Settings.

Clearing app data can remove saved levels, coin balances, preferences, and other progress. Make sure you understand the consequences before taking that action. Clearing the cache alone is not necessarily the same as clearing saved game data.

Review backup copies separately. Reinstalling or restoring a device may restore information where an enabled backup service supports it. We cannot recover local-only progress that has been permanently deleted and was not otherwise backed up.

Request deletion of information held by us

To request deletion of information you have sent us, email rajesh@monovatech.com with the subject: Traffic Arrow Puzzle Escape — Data Deletion Request .

Identify the message or information concerned. Where possible, use the email address associated with the original communication. We may request limited additional information where necessary and legally appropriate to verify the request.

Uninstalling the Game does not delete emails previously sent to us or automatically submit a deletion request. We cannot remotely retrieve or erase game records that are stored only on your device.

Where a lawful retention exception applies, we will explain the relevant reason to the extent permitted by law. The covered configuration has no Game accounts, but requests remain available for information separately held by us.
