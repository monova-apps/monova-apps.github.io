1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to Flag Keyboard: Country Themes (the “Application”), operated by Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd., Birtamod Municipality-4, Jhapa, Nepal (“we”, “us”, or “our”). The Application provides an Android keyboard with country flag-inspired appearance options.

This policy covers keyboard access, local settings, advertising, support, and privacy choices. Optional features apply only where included in your release. We act as controller for information whose purposes and means of processing we determine.

2. Keyboard Input and Sensitive Information

When selected as your input method, the Application processes key presses and composed text and delivers input to the active app and text field through Android.

Android may expose limited editor information, such as input type, selected or surrounding text, cursor position, and the app requesting input. Access depends on Android and the editor. It supports typing rather than unrestricted access to other apps, messages, or historical activity.

We do not retain a complete typing history, upload typed text or editor contents, or provide them to advertising services. The app receiving your input independently determines how it uses and stores that text.

Input may include passwords, payment details, or private messages. The keyboard necessarily handles entered characters, but we do not separately collect or retain them as a record. Sensitive-field behaviour also depends on how the receiving app identifies its fields; we do not promise that the input method is technically unable to encounter sensitive content.

3. Themes, Preferences, and Optional Features

Theme choices, layouts, languages, sound, vibration, keyboard size, and available preferences may be saved locally. Selecting a country flag is an appearance preference and does not establish your nationality, ethnicity, citizenship, or location. We do not use that selection to infer sensitive identity attributes.

If suggestions or a personal dictionary are included, processing and any supported learning remain on-device. There is no cloud prediction service in the workflow described here. Learned words may contain personal information and can be removed using available reset controls or app-data deletion.

If clipboard features are available, clipboard text is processed for actions you request under Android’s access restrictions. Supported saved clips remain local until removed. Clipboard contents are not supplied to advertisers.

If custom backgrounds are available, selected images are used locally for personalisation. An app-specific copy may persist; deleting it does not necessarily delete the original image.

4. Permissions and Activation

You must enable and select the keyboard through Android input-method settings. Installation alone does not make it your active keyboard. Android may display a general warning about third-party keyboard access.

Android’s BIND_INPUT_METHOD protection restricts connections to the keyboard service. It is not a general permission to read contacts, messages, files, or other apps.

Network and advertising-related access supports AdMob. An image picker or media permission may support an included custom-background feature. The typing workflow does not require camera access, microphone recording, contacts, SMS, call history, or precise GPS. A separate voice-input service, if invoked, has its own provider, permissions, and privacy terms.
