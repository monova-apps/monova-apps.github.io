8. Your Privacy Rights

Your rights depend on your location, the applicable law, and the processing involved. They are subject to the conditions and exceptions established by that law.

European data-protection rights

Where the General Data Protection Regulation applies, rights may include access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, withdrawal of consent, and safeguards concerning certain automated decisions. You may also complain to the competent supervisory authority. Further information is available from the European Commission.

California privacy rights

Where the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended, applies to our processing, eligible California residents may have rights to know and access information, correct inaccuracies, request deletion, opt out of sale or qualifying sharing, limit certain uses of sensitive information, and exercise rights without unlawful discrimination.
