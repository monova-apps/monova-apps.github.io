This Privacy Policy explains how Monova Tech ("Service Provider", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Calendar Planner mobile application (the "Application").

Information Collection and Use

Calendar Planner allows you to organize your schedule, create events, set reminders, and manage your personal plans.

Depending on the features you use, information may include:

  • Calendar events and event titles.
  • Event dates and times.
  • Reminders and notification settings.
  • Notes or descriptions associated with events.
  • Recurring event information.
  • Calendar preferences and application settings.
Calendar information: Information you enter into Calendar Planner is used to provide calendar and planning functionality. Calendar information is not sold by Monova Tech.

Calendar Permission

If the Application requests access to your device's calendar, that permission is used only to provide calendar-related functionality that you choose to use.

Calendar access is controlled by your device's operating system. You can manage or revoke the Application's calendar permission through your device settings.

If you do not grant calendar permission, features requiring access to your device calendar may not be available.

Notifications and Reminders

Calendar Planner may request notification permission to remind you about scheduled events, tasks, appointments, or other planned activities.

Notifications are used to provide reminder functionality and can be disabled through your device settings.

Information Stored on Your Device

Calendar Planner may store calendar entries, preferences, reminders, and other application data locally on your device.

Local data remains on your device unless you choose to remove it or the Application provides a feature that synchronizes or transfers it.

Uninstalling the Application or clearing its application data may permanently remove locally stored Application information.
