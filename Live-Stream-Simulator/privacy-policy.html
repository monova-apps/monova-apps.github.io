Privacy Policy

Live Stream Simulator

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Live Stream Simulator, provided by Monova Tech, handles information when you use the app. The app provides a simulated live-streaming experience for entertainment purposes. It does not represent a real broadcast unless a feature explicitly states otherwise.

Information Collection

The app does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location.

Simulated Streaming Features

Any simulated viewers, comments, likes, live notifications, streaming statistics, or similar activity displayed in the app are intended for entertainment and simulation. These elements should not be interpreted as actual audience activity or engagement on a real streaming platform.

Camera and Microphone Access

If a feature you use requires camera or microphone access, the app will use the relevant permission to provide that feature. Camera and microphone access is not required for features that do not use them. The app does not intentionally upload captured media or audio to Monova Tech servers.

Data Storage

App settings, preferences, and simulation progress may be stored locally on your device when needed for the app's functionality. This information is not intentionally uploaded to Monova Tech servers and may be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Data Sharing

Monova Tech does not intentionally sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties through the app's core simulation features. The app does not require you to connect a real streaming account to use its simulated features.

Third-Party Services

If third-party services are included in the app, such as advertising or analytics services, those providers may process information as described in their own privacy policies. Please review the disclosures applicable to the version of the app you use.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. You are also responsible for maintaining the security of your device and any information stored on it.

Children's Privacy

The app does not intentionally collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published with a revised effective date.

Your Consent

By using Live Stream Simulator, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
