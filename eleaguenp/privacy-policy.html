Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us" or "our"), the operator of EleagueNp, collects, uses, shares and protects personal information, and the choices available to you.

It applies to the EleagueNp website at eleaguenp.com, its iPhone Home Screen web app, and any EleagueNp mobile application that links to this policy (together, the "Service"). A feature described as optional or conditional applies only where it is available in the version you use.

EleagueNp connects competitors for matches played in third-party games. We handle game-account registration, challenges, match-room coordination, evidence review, entry payments, approved prizes, withdrawals, referrals and support. We do not operate the underlying games. Their publishers process information under their own policies.

At a glance
  • We use account and game details to arrange matches and verify that participants use their registered game identities.
  • Match opponents and authorised administrators may see relevant game details and result evidence. Rankings display competition information publicly.
  • Payments, screenshots, QR codes and support uploads may contain personal information. Submit only what is necessary.
  • The app may contain ads. Advertising features may involve third-party providers and additional privacy choices, as explained below.
  • Contact rajesh@monovatech.com for privacy questions, access, correction or account-deletion requests.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

When you register or use an account, we process your name, email address, account identifier, account creation and update dates, authentication information, account status and relevant preferences. Passwords are stored as password hashes rather than as readable passwords. We also maintain administrative permissions and account restriction records where applicable.

Game identities

We process the game you select, your in-game username, player ID and base team name where requested. Older records may retain a previously supplied game-account level. We may retain the time an identity was registered or locked, corrections made by support, and the identity snapshot associated with each match. We do not ask for your personal Free Fire, PUBG Mobile or eFootball account password.

Competition activity

We record challenges you create or join, game modes, team formats, opponents, entry amounts, entry tickets used, room identifiers, readiness and countdown timestamps, match status, scores, claimed outcomes, approved wins, disputes and administrative decisions. Where provided for a match, we process external game-room IDs and room passwords. These are different from your personal game-account credentials.

Evidence and support content

We process screenshots, images, videos, messages and other information you submit for payment verification, match results, disputes or support. Files can contain visible names, game IDs, transaction details, other participants and embedded metadata. Chat supports image and video attachments subject to the limits displayed in the Service, currently up to 10 MB per attachment. Do not assume that all embedded metadata is automatically removed.

Financial and referral records

We process wallet balances and held amounts, deposit and withdrawal requests, payment method, amount, transaction reference, payment proof, recipient account name and number, payout QR codes, approval or rejection details, refunds, prize credits, commissions and balance adjustments. We also process referral codes, referral relationships, qualification status, claim history, referral credits and entry-ticket issuance, value and use.

Technical information

Our hosting and delivery services may process IP addresses, browser or app version, device and operating-system information, request times, error details, access logs and security-related events when you connect. Optional advertising or diagnostic features may process additional information as described when enabled. This policy does not mean that every category is collected by every version.

Information from others

We may receive information about you from an opponent's result submission, a referral relationship, an administrator's review, support correspondence or a payment-verification record. We do not need access to your device address book for you to share a referral link.

2. How We Use Information

  • Create accounts, authenticate users and provide account support.
  • Register game identities, find compatible challenges, coordinate rooms and communicate match details.
  • Review evidence, resolve conflicting results, maintain rankings and determine approved prizes or refunds.
  • Verify deposits, administer wallet balances and entry tickets, review withdrawals and reconcile payment records.
  • Track referral eligibility, prevent duplicate claims and issue claimed rewards.
  • Answer support requests and display service announcements and account notifications.
  • Detect impersonation, duplicate payments, fraudulent evidence, unauthorised account use and other abuse.
  • Maintain the Service, investigate errors and protect its reliability and security.
  • Provide and measure advertising where enabled and permitted by your choices and applicable requirements.
  • Meet applicable recordkeeping obligations, respond to lawful requests and establish or defend legal claims.

Matchmaking and referral calculations may use automated rules. Submitted match evidence and financial requests are subject to the administrative review described in the Service. Contact support if you believe a record or decision is incorrect.

3. Grounds for Processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we process information as necessary to provide the Service you request and perform our agreement with you; meet legal obligations; pursue legitimate interests such as security, fraud prevention and resolving disputes where those interests are not overridden by your rights; or with your consent where required.

Optional advertising identifiers, tracking, marketing or device access may require separate permission or consent. Reading this policy, creating an account or continuing to use the Service does not by itself replace any specific consent required by law. You may withdraw consent for future processing through the available controls or by contacting us. Withdrawal does not invalidate processing lawfully undertaken before it.

4. Game Identities, Match Evidence and Visibility

Open-room listings may show the host's display name or game username, selected game, mode, team format, entry fee, prize and room status. Match participants can see the registered identities and match information needed to compete and review results. Relevant evidence may be visible to the opposing participant so that a result can be confirmed or disputed.

Public leaderboards may show game display names, rankings, approved win totals and the applicable ranking period. Your financial records, payment account details and private support conversations are not intended for public leaderboards.

Use the game identity registered for that match. A different game ID can invalidate a win claim under the match rules. We keep historical identity snapshots so that later profile changes do not alter the evidence of who entered an earlier match. Contact support to correct a locked identity.

External game-room credentials are provided to participants when published by an administrator. Keep them confidential. If you record suspected manipulation or cheating, limit the recording to relevant gameplay and comply with applicable recording rules. Remove unrelated private chats, passwords, one-time codes and personal information before uploading.

5. Payments, Wallets, Entry Tickets and Referrals

EleagueNp maintains an account balance and transaction history for entries, approved prizes and withdrawals. Entry tickets are recorded separately from cash balances and cover eligible entries under the displayed rules. Referral tracking records the relationship between accounts and whether the applicable qualification condition has been met; claiming a reward also records the referral credits consumed.

For deposits and withdrawals, payment services such as eSewa or Khalti process their own payment information under their policies. We receive the details you submit and the verification or payout records needed to reconcile the request. Never provide a payment-service password, PIN, OTP or full card security details in a screenshot or chat.

A referral link identifies its referrer. A referrer may see progress or qualification information exposed by the reward feature. Sharing a referral link does not authorise access to another player's private payment proof or account credentials.

Deleting an account does not automatically erase records that remain necessary to reconcile funds, investigate fraud, resolve disputes or satisfy applicable financial and legal obligations.

6. When We Share Information

We share information where necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including with:

  • Authorised staff: administrators and support personnel who review accounts, game identities, payments, evidence, disputes and messages.
  • Hosting, database and media providers: infrastructure services that store and deliver the Service. Uploaded images and videos may be processed by Cloudinary. A media URL may allow anyone who obtains it to access that file; do not share evidence or payment-proof links publicly.
  • Telegram: configured administrative bots may send player details, deposit proof, withdrawal information, match entries, scores, result screenshots, approved outcomes and signup alerts to administrator destinations. Telegram processes these messages on its infrastructure. Copies may exist outside the EleagueNp database, and deleting an app record does not itself delete every Telegram copy.
  • Payment providers: where needed to verify or complete the payment or withdrawal you request.
  • Other participants and the public: only the relevant competition information described in Section 4.
  • Advertising providers: where advertising is enabled, subject to Section 7 and required choices.
  • Authorities and professional advisers: where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, investigate abuse or handle legal claims.
  • A successor operator: in a merger, acquisition or transfer of the Service, subject to applicable confidentiality, notice and data-protection requirements.

We do not sell payment proofs, private support attachments or game-account credentials to advertisers. Advertising identifiers and activity, if shared by an enabled advertising integration, are addressed separately below. Third-party recipients may have independent responsibilities under their own privacy policies.

Provider information: Cloudinary Privacy Policy and Telegram Privacy Policy.

7. Advertising — The App May Contain Ads

EleagueNp may contain advertisements, including promotional banners, external offers or ads supplied by third-party advertising services. Ad availability and format may differ between the website, web app and mobile releases.

A promotional banner and an advertising SDK are different features. This policy does not assert that Google AdMob or any other specific advertising SDK is currently installed. When a third-party advertising integration is introduced, the applicable provider information and any required in-app notices and consent controls must be supplied before the relevant collection begins.

Where an advertising service is enabled, it may process an advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, device and operating-system information, app information, ad impressions, clicks, interactions and diagnostic or fraud-prevention signals. Advertising may be contextual or personalised where legally permitted and consistent with your choices.

Where required, we request consent before non-essential advertising storage, personalisation or tracking. On Apple devices, tracking across other companies' apps and websites requires the applicable system permission when the platform's rules require it. We do not use denial of tracking permission as permission to obtain the same identifier by another method.

You may use available privacy or ad controls and your device's advertising settings to limit personalisation, revoke permission or reset/delete an advertising identifier where supported. These choices may not remove all ads. Non-personalised ads can still require limited information for delivery, security, frequency control and measurement, subject to applicable requirements.

We do not provide wallet balances, payment QR codes, payment screenshots, private chats or match-evidence uploads as inputs for personalised advertising. Clicking an ad can take you to an external service with its own privacy practices. In jurisdictions that classify certain advertising disclosures as a sale, sharing or targeted advertising, applicable opt-out rights will be honoured when those activities occur.

8. Cookies, Local Storage and Home Screen Installation

The web Service uses browser storage for authentication tokens and relevant preferences. Its service worker may cache public app resources, icons and an offline page so that the app shell can load efficiently. Offline availability does not mean that current wallet information, match state or payment operations can be used without a server connection.

Installing the iPhone Home Screen web app does not grant access to your other apps, contacts, private files or local network. Clearing browser storage can sign you out or reset preferences. Uninstalling a Home Screen icon or a native app does not delete your server-side account.

Hosting or embedded services may use additional cookies or similar technologies. Non-essential advertising or analytics storage, if introduced, is subject to the notices and consent choices required in your location. This standalone policy document has no scripts, tracking pixels, external fonts or embedded advertisements; its hosting service may separately maintain connection logs.

9. Device Permissions

When you choose to attach a screenshot, QR code, photo or video, the Service uses the file or media access provided by your browser or operating system. If a version offers capture from a camera or microphone, access should be requested when you use that feature. Access to selected content does not mean we need unrestricted access to your entire photo library.

Notification permission may be requested where device notifications are supported and you choose to enable them. An in-app announcement does not itself require system notification permission. You can change permissions in device or browser settings, although the affected optional feature may then be unavailable.

The core Service does not require your contacts, SMS messages, precise GPS location, other-app contents or access to services on your local network. We do not continuously record your screen or gameplay; evidence is provided through your submissions. A future feature that needs additional access must explain its purpose before requesting it.

10. How Long We Keep Information

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, taking account of account activity, unresolved matches, payment reconciliation, disputes, fraud risk, legal obligations and limitation periods. Different categories therefore have different retention needs:

  • Account and game profiles: while your account is active and afterwards where necessary to address a valid request or outstanding obligation.
  • Wallet, payment and settlement records: for applicable accounting, regulatory, dispute-resolution and fraud-prevention needs, including after account closure where required.
  • Evidence and support attachments: while needed to handle the relevant case and any related review, appeal or legal claim.
  • Referral and ticket records: while needed to administer outstanding rewards, establish redemption history and prevent duplicate claims.
  • Technical and security records: for operational troubleshooting, security investigations and applicable legal needs.

When information is no longer needed, it should be deleted or irreversibly de-identified. Where we retain records after a deletion request, we will explain the applicable reason and relevant retention period or criteria. Backup copies and external provider copies may remain until their applicable deletion or replacement process completes; they must not be used to restore an active account against a valid deletion request except where required for a lawful purpose.

11. Your Choices, Rights and Account Deletion

You can review available account information through your profile, wallet, match history and rewards pages. Contact support for corrections to locked game identities or historical records. Changes to a current profile may not overwrite the original record of an earlier transaction or match.

Depending on the law applicable to you, you may request access to personal information, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, a portable copy, withdrawal of consent or an objection to particular processing. These rights are subject to applicable exceptions. You may also complain to the competent data-protection or other authority in your jurisdiction.

How to request deletion

Email rajesh@monovatech.com with the subject “EleagueNp account deletion”, preferably from your registered email address. You may also contact EleagueNp support while signed in. The email route does not require you to keep the app installed or remain signed in.

Tell us whether you want to delete your account and associated personal information or request deletion of specific content. Do not send your password, OTP or payment PIN. We may ask for proportionate information to verify ownership before disclosing or deleting data. We will respond within the period required by applicable law and explain any lawful extension or refusal.

We will review outstanding funds, entries, disputes and recordkeeping requirements when handling closure. Records that must be retained for a lawful reason are handled separately from the active account and kept only for that purpose. Account deletion is different from logging out or uninstalling the app. We cannot erase copies that another participant has independently saved, but this does not remove our responsibility to handle information under our control and coordinate applicable deletion requests with our providers.

12. Children and Eligibility

EleagueNp's paid-entry competition and wallet features are intended for adults aged 18 or older, or any higher minimum age required in the user's location. They are not intended for children. This statement is not a claim that every user has undergone documentary age verification.

If you believe a child has provided personal information or opened an account contrary to these restrictions, contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate steps, including account restriction and deletion of information not lawfully required to be retained. A parent or guardian may contact us about information concerning their child. This privacy policy does not establish that paid competitions are permitted in every location.

13. Security

We use measures appropriate to the Service, including HTTPS connections, password hashing, authenticated access and administrative access controls. Nevertheless, no internet transmission, storage system, media-sharing link or administrative process can be guaranteed completely secure.

Protect your password and device, log out on shared devices and avoid sending unrelated sensitive information in evidence or support uploads. Support messages and Telegram administrative workflows should not be treated as end-to-end encrypted confidential vaults. Report suspected account compromise promptly. Where a personal-data breach triggers legal notification duties, the required notifications will be made.
