Privacy Policy

Screenshot Cleaner

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Screenshot Cleaner, provided by Monova Tech, handles information when you use the app. Screenshot Cleaner helps users find, review, organize, and remove screenshots from their devices.

Photo and Media Access

The app may request access to photos or images on your device to identify and display screenshots for cleaning. Access is used to provide the app's core functionality and depends on the permissions you grant through Android.

Screenshot Processing

Screenshots and related image information are processed on your device to help you review and manage unwanted screenshots. The app does not intentionally upload your screenshots or image content to Monova Tech servers.

Deleting Screenshots

If you choose to delete screenshots, the app uses the relevant Android system permissions or confirmation process. Deleted images may not be recoverable. Please review selected files carefully before confirming deletion.

Information Collection

The app does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location through its core cleaning features.

Data Storage

App settings, preferences, and any locally maintained cleaning information may be stored on your device. Your screenshots remain in your device's storage unless you choose to delete them or move them using available device features.

Data Sharing

Monova Tech does not intentionally sell, rent, or share your personal information or screenshot content with third parties through the app's core cleaning features. Screenshot content is not intentionally transmitted to Monova Tech servers.

Third-Party Services

If the version of the app you use includes third-party services, such as advertising or analytics, those providers may process certain information according to their own privacy policies. Any such practices should be described in the app's applicable privacy disclosures.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. You are responsible for reviewing files before deleting them and for maintaining the security of your device and its stored content.

Children's Privacy

Screenshot Cleaner does not intentionally collect personal information from children through its core features. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published with a revised effective date.

Your Consent

By using Screenshot Cleaner, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
