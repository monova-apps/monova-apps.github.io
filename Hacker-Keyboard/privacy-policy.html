Privacy Policy

Hacker Keyboard

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Hacker Keyboard, provided by Monova Tech, handles information when you use the app. Hacker Keyboard offers themed keyboard designs and typing customization features inspired by hacker-style aesthetics.

Keyboard Access

As a keyboard app, Hacker Keyboard may need to be enabled in your device's keyboard settings before you can use it to type. A keyboard can potentially process sensitive information, including passwords, messages, and other text entered by users. We take this responsibility seriously.

Typed Text and Personal Information

Hacker Keyboard does not intentionally collect, record, or transmit the text you type, including passwords, messages, or other sensitive information, to Monova Tech servers. Do not enter sensitive information into any feature unless you understand how that feature handles your data.

Themes and Customization

The app may store keyboard themes, appearance settings, preferences, and other customization choices locally on your device. These settings are used to provide your preferred keyboard experience.

Information Collection

The app does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location through its core keyboard features.

Internet Access and Data Transmission

Hacker Keyboard does not intentionally transmit typed text or personal information to Monova Tech servers. If the app version you use includes online features, advertising, analytics, or third-party services, those services may process certain information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Storage and Retention

Keyboard themes, settings, and preferences may be stored locally on your device. You can remove locally stored app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app, where applicable. Text entered through the keyboard may also be handled by the app in which you are typing.

Data Sharing

Monova Tech does not intentionally sell or rent your personal information. Typed text is not intentionally shared with Monova Tech by the core keyboard functionality. Any third-party services included in the app may have separate data practices.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. However, no software or data storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should review Android's keyboard selection settings and enable only keyboards you trust.

Children's Privacy

Hacker Keyboard does not intentionally collect personal information from children through its core keyboard features. If you believe that personal information has been provided through the app, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published with a revised effective date.

Your Consent

By using Hacker Keyboard, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
