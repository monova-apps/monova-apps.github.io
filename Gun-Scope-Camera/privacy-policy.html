Gun Scope Camera: Zoom FX overlays scope and reticle graphics and lens effects on your live camera preview, photos, and videos. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Short version: Your camera and microphone processing happens on your device. The app does not send your photos, videos, or personal data to us or to anyone else.

Last updated: 3 November 2026

1. Summary

  • The app does not send your photos, videos, or other personal data to us or to anyone else.
  • The app does not require an account.
  • The app does not include analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs.
  • All camera and microphone processing happens on your device.

2. Data the App Accesses

Data Why it is used Where it is stored
Camera feed Shows the live scope preview and applies reticle and lens effects. Frames are processed in real time and are not saved unless you capture a photo or video. Not stored unless you capture media.
Microphone audio Recorded only while you record a video, so the video can include sound. Saved inside the video file you create.
Photos and videos you capture Saved so you can view, share, or delete them in the app's gallery. On your device, in Pictures/Gun Scope Camera or Movies/Gun Scope Camera, through Android MediaStore.
App settings Remembers your selected scope, reticle size and opacity, selected lens effect, and acceptance of the disclaimer. On your device only, using Android DataStore.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: Required to show the live preview and capture photos and videos.
  • Microphone: Required to record audio with videos.
  • Storage (Android 9 and below only): Required to save captures to the device's media library. On Android 10 and above, the app saves through MediaStore and does not need storage permission.

4. Data Transmission and Third Parties

The app does not send your photos, videos, camera frames, microphone audio, settings, or other personal data to us or to anyone else. The app does not include analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs.

When you choose to share a photo or video using another app or service, that service may process the media according to its own privacy policy.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Captures remain on your device until you delete them in the app's gallery or with a file manager.
  • Settings remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its settings. Captured files in Pictures/Gun Scope Camera or Movies/Gun Scope Camera remain until you delete them.

6. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app does not create online accounts or profiles.

7. Security

Because data stays on your device, its safety depends on your device's security settings, such as a screen lock and device encryption. We recommend keeping your device protected.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the most recent revision. Please review this policy when an updated version is published.

9. Entertainment Use

Gun Scope Camera: Zoom FX is a visual entertainment and creative photo and video app. It is not a firearm, rangefinder, targeting device, or ballistic tool, and it provides no targeting or ranging data.
