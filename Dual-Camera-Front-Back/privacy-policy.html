1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to Dual Camera: Front & Back (the “Application”), operated by Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd., Birtamod Municipality-4, Jhapa, Nepal (“we”, “us”, “our”, or the “Service Provider”).

The Application enables simultaneous recording using the front-facing and rear-facing cameras on compatible Android devices. It processes both camera views to provide the preview and recording output selected through its available controls.

This policy explains how information is accessed, processed, stored, and disclosed when you use the Application, interact with advertisements, or contact us. It distinguishes local camera and media processing from advertising information transmitted to service providers and information you separately send to us.

Camera compatibility, recording layouts, export options, and permission requirements depend on the installed release, device hardware, and Android version. References to optional features apply where those features are included and used.

We are responsible for personal information whose purposes and means of processing we determine, including support correspondence and privacy requests. Where applicable law uses the term “controller”, we act as controller for that processing.

2. Information We Handle

Simultaneous front and rear camera access

When you activate dual-camera features, the Application accesses both the front and rear cameras concurrently. It processes frames from both cameras to display previews and create recordings you initiate.

Both views can contain personal information. The front camera may capture your face and nearby people, while the rear camera may capture your surroundings, documents, screens, addresses, or other private details.

For the local recording workflow described here, camera frames and recordings are processed on your device. They are not automatically uploaded to our servers or supplied to Google AdMob as advertising content. We do not receive a remote live feed from either camera.

Recorded videos and output files

The Application processes both camera streams to create the output supported by your selected recording settings. Depending on the available features, this can involve arranging camera views, combining frames, encoding video, preparing previews, and saving output files.

A saved recording may contain both your own image and the scene facing away from you. Review both views before saving or sharing sensitive recordings.

Microphone audio

Where sound recording is available and enabled, the Application accesses microphone audio after you grant the required permission. Audio may include your voice, other people’s voices, and surrounding sounds, and may be included in the resulting video.

Denying microphone access may prevent sound recording or limit recording features that depend on it. Camera permission alone does not grant microphone access.

Selected media and optional image features

If your installed release offers media import, playback, image capture, or editing features, the Application accesses files you select or authorise it to access for the requested operation.

Access through a system picker can be limited to selected files. The scope of any broader media permission depends on Android and the access you grant.

Metadata, settings, and temporary files

Recordings and associated files may include file names, creation dates, duration, dimensions, orientation, frame rate, encoding information, and recording settings. Imported media may contain metadata added by another app or device, including location information.

The Application may store available recording preferences, camera-view arrangements, thumbnails, cached previews, and temporary media used to process or save recordings. Exporting or sharing media does not guarantee that all metadata is removed.

Advertising-related information

The Application uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Depending on the SDK version, configuration, device settings, consent choices, and applicable restrictions, advertising processing can include:

  • Network information: IP address and connection information. An IP address may be used to estimate general location.
  • Identifiers: Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other supported device or account-related identifiers where applicable and permitted.
  • Device and application details: device model, operating-system version, app version, language, and relevant technical settings.
  • Interactions: ad requests, impressions, clicks, video views, app launches, taps, and other interactions measured by the advertising SDK.
  • Diagnostics: SDK errors, crash information, performance measurements, and related technical information.
  • Privacy preferences: consent status and signals used to apply advertising choices and regional restrictions.

Advertising information may be transmitted to Google and relevant advertising service partners separately from local camera processing. Local recording does not mean the Application has no network activity or advertising-related data collection.

Support and privacy communications

When you contact us, we receive your email address, message content, attachments you choose to send, and ordinary email delivery information. You may voluntarily include your device model, Android version, app version, or a description of a compatibility or recording issue.

Please avoid sending private recordings, identity documents, passwords, or unrelated sensitive information. If a sample recording is necessary, remove unrelated personal details from both camera views and the audio before sending it.

3. Device Permissions

Camera permission

Camera permission is used to access the front and rear cameras, display previews, and record both views at the same time on compatible devices. Declining or revoking permission prevents camera features from working.

Android may provide a single camera permission covering access to multiple device cameras. Granting that permission enables the dual-camera access required by this feature; it does not authorise unrelated uploading or advertising use of your recordings.

Microphone permission

Microphone access is used to include sound in recordings when that feature is enabled. It is separate from camera access and remains subject to your permission choices and Android restrictions.

Photos, videos, and storage

Access needed to open or save media depends on Android version, storage location, and implementation. The Application may use a system picker, shared media storage, or a media or storage permission where required.

Saving a newly recorded video and browsing existing media are different operations. Some save operations do not require broad access to your existing library.

Notifications and foreground services, where applicable

An included recording or export feature may use a foreground service and status notification to support an operation you initiate. Camera or microphone use while the interface is not visible depends on the feature, permissions, and Android restrictions.

Camera and microphone permissions do not authorise covert recording. Use the available controls to start and stop your recording sessions.

Network and advertising access

Internet access, network-state information, and advertising identifier access may be used for AdMob delivery, measurement, security, and related permitted processing. Some permissions are granted automatically rather than through a runtime prompt.

Other sensitive access

The dual-camera workflow described here does not require access to contacts, SMS, call history, or precise GPS location. General location inferred from an IP address is separate from GPS access.

Managing permissions

You can review and change available permissions in Android Settings. Revoking permission limits future access covered by it but does not necessarily delete files already saved or information already transmitted.

Device permissions do not replace separate advertising consent or other disclosures required by applicable law.

7. Storage and Retention

Recordings and exported files

Recordings saved to shared storage or your gallery remain until you or another authorised app or service removes them. Uninstalling the Application does not necessarily delete files saved outside app-specific storage.

Where a recording session creates multiple output files, each file may need to be deleted separately. Cloud copies, backups, and files held by recipients have their own retention rules.

Settings and temporary media

Preferences, thumbnails, cached previews, and temporary recording files may remain until replaced, cleaned up, cleared through Android storage controls, or removed during uninstall. Interrupted recording or export operations may leave incomplete files.

Clearing app data may permanently remove app-specific information and media not saved elsewhere. Device backup or transfer services may preserve or restore some data depending on configuration.

Support and privacy records

We retain identifiable correspondence while resolving your request and necessary follow-up. Afterwards, information is retained only where necessary for an unresolved matter, legal obligation, or legal claim.

Limited records of privacy requests and responses may be retained to demonstrate appropriate handling. Information no longer needed is deleted or irreversibly anonymised.

Advertising information

Google and relevant advertising providers retain information according to their policies, purposes, and legal requirements. Retention varies by category and purpose.

Clearing app data, deleting an advertising identifier, or uninstalling the Application does not necessarily erase information already received by those providers.
