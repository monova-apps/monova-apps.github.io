1. Introduction

2. Sticker Content and Offline Use

Images and sticker projects

When you use a feature that creates stickers from images, the Application processes the content you provide and the editing choices needed for that feature. Depending on the implemented tools, project information can include source images, cut-outs, text, editing instructions, and exported sticker files.

Photographs and captions may identify people or reveal private information. Turning a photograph into a sticker, removing its background, or resizing it does not necessarily make the content anonymous. Use care when creating stickers featuring other people.

Sticker packs and metadata

Where pack creation is supported, a pack may have a name, author label, preview image, and associated sticker files. These details can reveal personal information when displayed or exported.

Source files can also contain metadata such as timestamps or location information added by another application. This policy does not promise automatic removal of all metadata from imported or exported files.
