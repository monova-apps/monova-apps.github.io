1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to Home Maintenance Reminder (the “Application”), operated by Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. (“we,” “us,” “our,” or the “Service Provider”).

It explains how information is accessed, used, stored, and disclosed in connection with the Application and communications with us. It covers the local-first Android app and distinguishes on-device processing from information received by the Service Provider.

We are responsible for personal information that we receive and determine how to use, including support correspondence and privacy requests. Where applicable law uses the term “data controller,” we act as controller for that processing.

2. Information We Handle

Maintenance records

The Application stores information you enter to organise maintenance, including task details, due dates, repeat intervals, completion history, appliance or vehicle details, warranty dates, model numbers, and notes.

Photos and attachments

You can add photos to relevant records, such as pictures of equipment, filters, receipts, repairs, or service documents.

Notes and photographs may contain personal information, including names, addresses, purchase details, or information about other household members. Add only what is necessary for your maintenance records and avoid storing passwords, access codes, or unnecessary identity documents.

A photograph may contain information within the image itself or in its metadata. Attaching it to a maintenance record does not automatically make that information anonymous or remove all metadata.

Information you send to us

When you email us, we receive your email address, message, any name or attachments you provide, and ordinary email delivery information. You may voluntarily include your device model, Android version, app version, or a description of an issue.

Before sending a screenshot, receipt, or other attachment, remove information unrelated to your request. We do not need your entire maintenance history to answer a general support question.

3. Permissions, Photos, and Reminders

Notifications

Notification access is used to display the reminders you configure. Android may require notification permission before alerts can appear. You can change notification settings through your device's app settings.

A reminder notification may reveal its title or other displayed details to someone viewing your screen. Review your lock-screen and notification-preview settings when reminders contain private information.

Disabling notifications can prevent reminder alerts from appearing. It does not, by itself, delete the underlying maintenance record.

Photo access

Access granted when adding a photo is used to attach and display that photo in the relevant record. The access mechanism depends on the installed version and Android: it may involve a system picker, selected-file access, or a relevant media permission.

When a direct camera option is available and used, any camera access requested is for capturing the photo you choose to attach. Providing a photo does not authorise unrelated use of your media.

A system picker may also show images supplied by a cloud-backed photo provider. Selecting one can involve that provider retrieving the image under its own privacy practices.

Managing access

You can review available permissions in Android settings. Declining or withdrawing access may prevent the related feature from working. Revoking a permission does not necessarily remove a copy that was previously attached to a record.

4. How Information Is Used

The information in your local records is used to organise tasks, calculate upcoming maintenance, display your records and attachments, and support the reminders you configure.

Information you separately send to us is used to respond to your message, investigate reported problems, handle privacy requests, and meet applicable legal obligations. Where necessary and lawful, it may also be used to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Our policy is not to use support correspondence to enrol you in marketing campaigns or create behavioural advertising profiles.

Legal grounds where applicable

Where a legal basis is required, ordinary support correspondence is handled on the basis of our legitimate interest in assisting users and resolving reported problems, subject to their rights and interests. Processing required by law relies on the relevant legal obligation.

Where a particular activity requires consent, consent must be obtained for that activity. You may withdraw it without affecting processing lawfully carried out beforehand. Installing the Application or continuing to use it does not replace affirmative consent where required.

5. Data Collection and External Services

Local app data

Our published Google Play Data safety declaration states that the Application does not collect data or share data with third parties. Your maintenance content is kept locally rather than automatically submitted to us.

Information processed on a device can still be personal information. The no-collection declaration does not mean that your notes or photographs contain no private details.

Support communications

Sending us an email is separate from using local app functions. Email delivery, storage, and support-administration providers process the information necessary to handle messages you choose to send.

Operating-system and store services

Google Play, Android, your device manufacturer, and services you independently use may process information for installation, updates, platform diagnostics, backups, or account security under their own privacy notices.

Independent platform processing is different from data collection by a service integrated into the Application. Any integrated service that transmits user or device information must be reflected in the Application's disclosures.

This privacy-policy page

This HTML document contains no scripts, tracking pixels, external fonts, or embedded advertising. The service hosting the page may separately process connection information, such as an IP address and browser request details. The document's contents do not determine the hosting service's logging practices.

6. When Information Is Disclosed

We do not sell your local maintenance content or provide it to advertisers. Information that remains solely on your device is not available to us to disclose.

Personal information that you separately provide to us may be disclosed in the following limited circumstances:

  • Support operations: To providers involved in delivering, storing, or helping us respond to your communication.
  • Legal requirements: Where necessary to comply with a binding legal obligation or valid legal process.
  • Rights and safety: Where necessary and legally permitted to protect people, investigate unlawful activity, or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
  • Business changes: In connection with a merger, acquisition, reorganisation, or transfer of relevant business assets, subject to applicable privacy requirements.
  • Your instructions: Where you specifically request or lawfully authorise a disclosure.

When you independently share a screenshot, photograph, or record with another person or service, that recipient receives the information you send. Deleting your local copy does not recall copies already shared.

7. Storage and Data Retention

Local records and attachments

Saved app information remains until you remove it through available app controls, clear the Application's storage, or otherwise delete it. Marking a task complete is different from deleting its service history.

Original photographs stored outside the Application may remain after an attachment or app record is removed. Likewise, deleting an original photograph may not remove a separate copy already saved with a record.

Device backups

Depending on the app's backup configuration, Android version, and your settings, device backup or transfer services may preserve or restore local app information. These services can store copies separately from the currently installed app.

Local-first storage does not, by itself, mean that operating-system backups are disabled. Manage relevant backup copies through your device or backup provider. We do not have access to your personal backup account merely because you use the Application.

Support correspondence

We retain identifiable correspondence while addressing your request and necessary follow-up. Further retention is limited to a specific continuing need, such as an unresolved dispute, an applicable legal obligation, or the establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims.

Information no longer needed for those purposes is to be deleted or irreversibly anonymised rather than kept indefinitely. A limited record of a privacy request and its outcome may be retained where necessary to demonstrate proper handling.

8. Your Choices and Data Deletion

Manage records on your device

Use available record-management controls to change or remove information. To remove app-specific information together, use Android's app-storage controls to clear storage or uninstall the Application. Menu names vary by device.

Clearing app storage can remove reminders, history, preferences, and locally saved attachments. Preserve anything you need before taking that action. Clearing the cache alone is not the same as deleting all app data.

Manage original photos, files saved elsewhere, backup copies, and copies sent to others separately. We cannot remotely retrieve or erase information stored only on your device.

Request deletion of information held by us

Email rajesh@monovatech.com with the subject Home Maintenance Reminder — Data Deletion Request .

Identify the communication or information concerned. Where possible, contact us from the email address used for the original communication. We may request limited additional information where necessary and legally appropriate to verify the request.

Uninstalling the Application does not erase emails previously sent to us or automatically submit a deletion request. Where a lawful retention exception applies, we will explain the relevant reason to the extent permitted by law.

You do not need to create an app account or send your entire maintenance database to submit a privacy request.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Your rights depend on your location, the processing involved, and applicable law. Rights are subject to the conditions and exceptions established by that law.

European data-protection rights

Where the General Data Protection Regulation applies, you may have rights to access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, withdrawal of consent, and safeguards concerning certain automated decisions. You may also complain to the competent supervisory authority.

California privacy rights

Where the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended, applies, eligible California residents may have rights to know and access information, correct inaccuracies, request deletion, opt out of sale or qualifying sharing, limit certain uses of sensitive information, and exercise rights without unlawful discrimination.

Submitting a request

Contact us using Section 14. We will address requests within applicable legal time limits. Verification will be proportionate and will not be imposed where prohibited. Authorised agents may act where permitted by law.

If a request cannot be fulfilled, we will explain the reason and any review or appeal mechanism required by applicable law.

10. Children's Privacy

Household records may contain information about family members, including children. Include only information necessary for the task and avoid attaching unrelated photographs or sensitive details.

We do not knowingly collect or retain personal information from children in circumstances that violate applicable children's privacy requirements.

A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent us information that should not be retained may contact us. We will review the concern and take the action required by law, including deletion or obtaining legally required authorisation where relevant.

11. International Processing

Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. is based in Nepal. Information you choose to send us may be accessed and handled in Nepal and processed by communication providers involved in delivering or storing your message.

This is separate from maintaining records on your device. Any international transfer subject to legal restrictions must satisfy the applicable requirements.

You may contact us for information about the recipients, processing locations, and safeguards relevant to personal information you have provided. Your own cloud storage or device-backup services operate under their respective arrangements.

12. Security and Personal-Data Incidents

Our policy is to restrict access to support and privacy correspondence to people and service providers who need it for the purposes described here. Safeguards must be appropriate to the information and risks involved.

Local records are also affected by the security of your device, its lock-screen settings, and any backups you use. Protect access to your device, especially when notes or photographs reveal household details.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure. This policy does not promise absolute security or a specific security certification.

If an incident affecting personal information under our control requires notification, we will notify the relevant authorities and affected individuals as required by applicable law.
