1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to Music player - Mp4 to Mp3 (the “Application”), operated by Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. (“we,” “us,” “our,” or the “Service Provider”).

It explains the handling of information when you use the Application in the configuration described above or communicate with us. It distinguishes information accessed or processed on your device from information received by the Service Provider.

We are responsible for personal information that we receive and determine how to use, including support messages and privacy requests. Where applicable law uses the term “data controller,” we act as controller for that processing.

2. Information We Handle

Audio files, videos, and media information

The Application accesses media you select or make available through the access granted on your device. This includes audio files for playback and MP4 files selected for conversion to MP3.

Relevant media information can include file names, file locations, file sizes, duration, format, title, artist, album information, and embedded artwork. The Application processes the information needed to identify, open, display, play, or convert the selected media.

Media can contain personal information, including voices, images, names, and descriptive metadata. Extracting audio from a video does not make the resulting audio anonymous.

Conversion output and temporary information

A conversion processes the selected video's audio content and produces an MP3 file. The process can involve local working data, temporary files, progress information, and an output file name and location.

In the configuration covered here, this processing takes place on the device. Source media, working data, and converted files are not uploaded to us or a remote conversion service.

Playback information and preferences

The Application uses the selected track, playback position, and player state to perform your playback choices. Any preferences or recent-file records saved by the covered configuration remain in local app storage, subject to device backup arrangements.

Communications you send to us

When you contact us, we receive your email address, message, attachments, and ordinary email delivery information. You may choose to include a device model, Android version, app version, or description of a problem.

Please avoid sending passwords, identity documents, private recordings, or complete media files unless they are necessary to resolve a specific issue. Remove unrelated personal information from screenshots or sample files before sending them.

3. Music Playback and MP4-to-MP3 Conversion

In the covered configuration, media is processed locally to play music and convert selected MP4 audio into MP3. The Application does not create a server-side copy of your music collection, conversion history, or converted files.

Converting an existing recording is different from recording your surroundings. This configuration does not capture microphone audio, telephone calls, or conversations.

The Application's local processing does not prevent a storage provider you choose from handling files. For example, selecting a file from a cloud-backed document provider or saving output to one can involve transfers performed by that provider.

Converted audio can reveal the same voices or other personal content as its source. File names and supported metadata may also reveal information. Review the output and its destination before sharing or uploading it elsewhere.

Deleting the source video does not necessarily delete an extracted MP3, and deleting the MP3 does not necessarily delete its source. Manage each retained copy separately.

4. Android Permissions and Device Access

Media access depends on the Android version and the file-access method used by the Application. Access may be provided through a system file picker, a music-and-audio or video permission, or a storage permission used on older Android versions.

These mechanisms are alternatives governed by the implementation and device. This policy does not imply that every permission is requested on every device.

Access is used to open selected media, obtain relevant media information, perform conversion, and save output where access permits. Declining or withdrawing access can prevent affected files from being opened or saved.

Where background playback or conversion is available, an operation you start may continue after leaving the app's screen. System controls or notifications can display track names, file names, or operation status. Consider your lock-screen settings when processing private media.

The covered configuration does not use microphone, camera, contacts, message, call-history, or precise location access for playback or conversion.

You can review available permissions in Android settings. Revoking access limits future access covered by that permission; it does not necessarily erase files or information already stored.

Platform information: Android media access and system file and document access .

5. How Information Is Used

Information processed locally is used to provide requested playback and conversion, display relevant media information, save output, and retain supported preferences. It is not used by us to profile your listening habits.

Information you separately send to us is used to respond to your communication, investigate reported issues, handle privacy requests, and comply with applicable legal obligations. Where necessary and legally permitted, it may also be used to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We do not use support correspondence to enrol you in marketing campaigns or build advertising profiles.

Legal grounds where required

Where a legal basis is required, responding to ordinary support requests is based on our legitimate interest in assisting users and resolving reported problems, subject to their rights and interests. Processing necessary to comply with an applicable legal obligation is based on that obligation.

Where an activity requires consent, that consent must relate to the activity concerned. You may withdraw consent without affecting processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal. Installing the Application or continuing to use it is not a substitute for affirmative consent where required.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Original media and converted files

Source files and exported MP3 files remain in their storage locations until deleted or otherwise managed by you or the relevant storage service. Files saved outside app-specific storage can remain after the Application is uninstalled.

Temporary files and local settings

Temporary conversion files and cached information may remain until removed through app cleanup, operating-system storage management, or your use of the device's cache or app-data controls. This policy does not promise immediate deletion after every conversion.

Saved preferences and other local app records remain until replaced, reset, or removed. Clearing app data can also remove app-specific outputs that have not been exported elsewhere.

Support and privacy-request records

Our retention policy is to keep correspondence while resolving the request and necessary follow-up. Afterwards, identifiable information is retained only where needed for an unresolved matter, an applicable legal obligation, or legal claims.

A limited record of a privacy request and our response may be retained where necessary to demonstrate proper handling. Unrelated information is not retained merely because a request was made. Information no longer needed for a legitimate retention purpose is to be deleted or irreversibly anonymised.

Backups

Depending on app configuration, Android version, and your settings, device backup or transfer services may preserve or restore local app data. Copies in backups or independently synchronised folders can have a separate lifecycle.

8. Your Choices and Data Deletion

Manage local information

Use Android settings to review permissions, clear cached data, clear app storage, or uninstall the Application. Available menu names vary by device. Preserve any files you need before clearing storage.

Use your device's file manager or the relevant storage provider to remove source media and exported MP3 files. Manage backups, shared copies, and cloud folders separately.

Removing an item from a player list or conversion history is not necessarily the same as deleting its underlying file. Revoking a permission also does not automatically delete previously created output.

Where a lawful retention exception applies, we will explain the relevant reason to the extent permitted by law. The covered configuration has no app accounts, but you can still request deletion of personal information separately held by us.
