Privacy Policy

Home Cheklist Reminder

Effective date: September 25, 2026
Last updated: September 25, 2026

Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. ("we," "us," or "our") develops and operates Home Cheklist Reminder (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App to manage household checklists, tasks, and reminders.

Your privacy at a glance: The App works without an account. Your checklists and reminders are stored on your device and are not sent to our servers. The App does not contain advertising or analytics trackers.

1. Information We Handle

The App stores information you enter or select, including:

  • Checklist names, task descriptions, and notes.
  • Task completion status.
  • Reminder dates, times, and repeat schedules.
  • App preferences and settings.

This information is processed locally on your device to provide the App's features. We do not receive or have remote access to your checklist or reminder content.

You do not need to provide your name, email address, phone number, or payment information to use the App. The App does not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or advertising identifier.

If you contact us for support, we receive the information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address, message, screenshots, and any technical details you choose to include. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed to resolve your request.

2. How Information Is Used

Information stored on your device is used to:

  • Create, display, edit, and organize checklists and tasks.
  • Schedule reminders and display local notifications.
  • Maintain task completion status.
  • Remember your preferences and settings.

Information you provide when contacting us is used to respond to your request, investigate technical issues, and maintain the security of our services.

We do not use your checklist content for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

3. Permissions and Notifications

The App may request notification permission and, where required by your device, permission to schedule alarms or reminders. These permissions are used to deliver reminders you create.

You can manage or revoke permissions through your device settings. Disabling notification or reminder permissions may prevent reminders from appearing or arriving at the scheduled time.

Notifications may display task names or reminder details on your lock screen. You can manage notification previews through your device settings.

4. Data Storage and Backups

Your checklists, tasks, reminders, and preferences are stored in the App's local storage on your device. We do not provide our own cloud synchronization or keep copies of this content on our servers.

Depending on your operating system, device settings, and the App's backup configuration, your device's backup or transfer service may include App data in a backup or move it to another device. These services are controlled by your device provider and are subject to its privacy policy.

We do not control or have access to these device backups.

5. Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell or rent personal information, share it for targeted advertising, or transmit your checklist content to third parties. The App does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.

If you contact us, our email or support provider may process your correspondence to deliver and manage support.

We may disclose information we actually hold, such as support correspondence, when required by applicable law or a valid legal request, or where necessary to protect rights and security. This does not give us access to information stored only on your device.

Your app store and operating system may independently process information under their own privacy policies, including information related to downloads, backups, and diagnostics you choose to share.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Local App data remains on your device until you delete it or remove the App's stored data.

  • You can delete individual checklists and tasks within the App.
  • You can remove locally stored App data using your device's clear-storage option, where available, or by uninstalling the App.
  • Copies in device backups must be managed separately through your backup provider's settings. Restoring a backup may restore previously saved App data.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve your request, address follow-up issues, or meet applicable legal obligations, and then delete it. You may contact us to request deletion of support information we hold.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information we handle. The security of locally stored App data also depends on your device's security controls.

No method of electronic storage or communication is completely secure. We encourage you to protect your device with a screen lock, keep its software updated, and avoid storing passwords or other highly sensitive information in checklist entries.

8. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You can view, edit, and delete your local checklist information within the App and manage permissions through your device settings. Because we do not receive your local App content, we cannot access, recover, or delete it remotely.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information we hold, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us to exercise applicable rights concerning your support correspondence.

You may also raise a concern with your local data protection authority.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is a general household organization tool and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information through a support request, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps to delete it.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App or our information practices. We will publish the updated policy and revise the date shown above.

If a change materially affects how personal information is handled, we will provide appropriate notice and obtain consent where required before introducing that processing.
