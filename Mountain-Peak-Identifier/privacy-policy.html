Privacy Policy

Mountain Peak Identifier

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Mountain Peak Identifier, provided by Monova Tech, handles information when you use the app. The app is designed to help users identify and explore mountain peaks using camera-based features.

Camera Access

Mountain Peak Identifier requires access to your device's camera to provide its mountain viewing and identification features. Camera data is used to display the camera view and support the app's functionality.

Photos and Videos

If the app allows you to capture photos or videos, the resulting media is saved to your device's local storage. The app does not intentionally upload your captured photos or videos to Monova Tech servers.

Location Information

Mountain Peak Identifier does not intentionally collect or store your precise location information. If location access is provided as an optional feature for improving mountain or geographic information, it is used only for that feature and is not intentionally stored by Monova Tech.

Information Collection

The app does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.

Data Storage

Photos, videos, app settings, and preferences may be stored locally on your device when required for the app's functionality. This information remains under your control and can be removed by deleting the relevant files or uninstalling the app.

Data Sharing

Monova Tech does not intentionally sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties through this app. Camera content and captured media are not intentionally transmitted to Monova Tech servers.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. Since captured media and certain app data may be stored on your device, you are also responsible for maintaining the security of your device and its storage.

Children's Privacy

Mountain Peak Identifier does not intentionally collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published with a revised effective date.

Your Consent

By using Mountain Peak Identifier, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
