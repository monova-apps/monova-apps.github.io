1. Introduction

It addresses information handled through photo-collage features, advertisements, integrated services, and communications you send to us. It distinguishes information processed on your device from information transmitted to us or another organisation.

2. Photos, Collage Projects, and Image Information

Photos you choose to use

When you select photographs for a collage, the Application processes those images to provide the creation and editing functions you use. Images can contain identifiable people, documents, addresses, or other private details.

Creating a collage does not make its source images anonymous. A combination of photographs may reveal more information about a person, family, event, or location than any individual image.

Project information and exported collages

Depending on the implemented features, project information can include selected images, layout choices, editing instructions, and text you add. Saving a project and exporting a finished image are different activities and may create separate files.

File information can include names, dimensions, timestamps, and embedded metadata. Existing photos may contain location metadata added by another application or device. This policy does not promise that metadata is automatically removed from every imported or exported image.
