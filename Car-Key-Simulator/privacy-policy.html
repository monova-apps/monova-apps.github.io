Privacy Policy

Car Key Simulator — Remote Prank

Last updated: 21 October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Car Key Simulator — Remote Prank. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information, what is stored on your device, which permissions are used, and how platform services may process technical information.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The app stores certain information locally to remember your preferences and provide its features. This information is kept in the app's private storage.

Information Storage Location Purpose
Preferences, including selected car, punchline, volume, and theme DataStore in the app's private storage To remember your settings between sessions
Number of pranks played DataStore in the app's private storage To determine when to ask you to rate the app
Generated prank cards The app's cache directory To temporarily prepare cards for sharing

The app itself does not transmit this locally stored information to an external server. Cached prank cards may be removed automatically by Android. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes its locally stored app data.

3. Diagnostics and App Usage Information

Android, Google Play services, or Google Play may process technical or diagnostic information related to app installation and use, depending on your device settings and the platform services involved. This information may include device or other identifiers, crash logs, and app interaction information where applicable.

Such information is handled by the relevant platform services under their own privacy policies and settings. The app's current implementation does not include its own analytics or crash reporting SDK.

4. Permissions

The app uses the following Android permission:

Permission Purpose
VIBRATE To reproduce the tactile feedback of a real car key fob.

The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, or internet permission.

5. Internet Access and Data Transmission

The current app implementation does not request the Android INTERNET permission. The app therefore does not use internet access to transmit its locally stored preferences, prank count, or generated prank cards to an external server.

Platform-level services may independently process technical or diagnostic information as described in the previous section.

6. Third-Party Services

The current implementation does not include third-party analytics or crash reporting SDKs. The app's sounds are generated by the app itself at runtime.

Android and Google Play platform services may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Please review the relevant provider's privacy information for further details.

7. Sharing Generated Content

If you choose to share a generated prank card using your device's sharing features, the selected application or service may receive the content you choose to share. Its use of that content is governed by its own privacy policy.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Preferences and the prank count remain in the app's private storage until they are changed or the app's data is cleared. Generated prank cards are stored temporarily in the cache and may be removed automatically by Android.

You can remove the app's locally stored data by clearing its data through Android settings or uninstalling the app.

9. Children's Privacy

Car Key Simulator — Remote Prank is designed to be suitable for users of all ages. The app itself does not collect personal information directly. Platform-level technical or diagnostic processing may still occur as described in this policy.

10. Information Security

The app stores its preferences and prank count in its private application storage. The app does not transmit this locally stored information over the internet. No method of electronic storage can guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or information practices change. Any changes will be published in this document and, where applicable, in the release notes for the relevant app version.

Please review this page periodically to stay informed about the app's privacy practices.
