Privacy Policy

Resume Builder and CV Generator

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Monova Tech handles information when you use the Resume Builder and CV Generator application. The Application allows you to create, edit, customize, and generate resumes and CVs using information you provide.

Information You Provide

The Application may allow you to enter information such as your name, contact details, education, work experience, skills, projects, and other resume content. This information is provided by you for the purpose of creating and customizing your resume or CV.

Resume and CV Data

Resume information entered into the Application is used to generate and display your resume or CV. Your resume content is intended to remain under your control and is not intentionally uploaded to Monova Tech's servers.

Device Storage

The Application may use your device's storage to save resume information, generated documents, templates, preferences, or other files required for its functionality. Locally stored files remain on your device unless you choose to share, export, move, or delete them.

File Access

If the Application provides options to import, export, or save resume documents, access to device storage may be required. Files are accessed only to provide the requested resume or CV functionality.

Information Collection

The Application does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information on Monova Tech's servers. Information entered for creating resumes is processed for the application's intended functionality.

Data Sharing

Monova Tech does not intentionally sell, rent, or share your resume information or personal information with third parties through the Application.

Data Storage and Retention

The Application does not intentionally store your resume content or personal information on Monova Tech's servers. Data saved by the Application remains on your device and can be removed by deleting the saved content, clearing application data, or uninstalling the Application.

Children's Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the Application, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the Application. Since resume information is primarily processed and stored locally on your device, you are responsible for protecting access to your device and any exported or shared resume files.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We recommend reviewing this Privacy Policy periodically.

Your Consent

By using Resume Builder and CV Generator, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
