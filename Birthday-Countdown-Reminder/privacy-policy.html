Information Collection and Use

Birthday Countdown & Reminder allows you to save birthdays, calculate countdowns, and receive reminders for important dates.

Depending on the features you use, information may include:

  • Names or nicknames of people you add.

Local information is used to display countdowns and schedule reminders. Depending on the Application's implementation, this information may remain on your device and may not be transmitted to the Service Provider.

Uninstalling the Application or clearing its application data may permanently remove locally stored information.

Notifications and Reminders

The Application may request permission to send notifications so that it can remind you about upcoming birthdays and other dates you choose to track.

Notifications are used solely to provide reminder functionality and can be disabled through your device settings.
