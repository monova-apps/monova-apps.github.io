1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to Parked Car finder (the “Application”), operated by Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. (“we,” “us,” “our,” or the “Service Provider”).

It explains information handling in connection with parking features, advertisements, and communications with us. Information stored on your device is distinguished from information transferred to us, an advertising provider, or another service.

Where applicable law uses the term “data controller,” we act as controller for personal information whose purposes and means of processing we determine. External providers may have different responsibilities depending on their actual services and contracts.

2. Parking Information

Parking records can include coordinates, a photo, parking level, row, zone, space number, notes, landmarks, timer settings, and parking history. The published parking feature stores these records on your device.

A saved parking position can reveal places you visit. Notes and photographs may reveal number plates, people, addresses, or other private details. Avoid including information unnecessary for finding your car.

Local storage of parking records does not establish that the advertising component sends no information off the device. Advertising is addressed separately in Section 5.

Information you send to us

When you contact us, we receive your email address, message, any name or attachments you provide, and ordinary email delivery information. You may voluntarily include device or app details to explain a problem.

Remove unrelated location information, private notifications, and sensitive details from screenshots before sending them. We do not need your entire parking history to answer a general support question.

3. Location Access and Navigation

The parking feature saves your position when you tap “Save My Car.” Its published behaviour does not involve background vehicle tracking.

Android can provide approximate or precise location, depending on the permission you grant and device settings. Approximate access can produce a less exact saved position. Declining location access prevents location-based functions from obtaining your position through that permission.

A parking-location permission does not constitute consent to use precise coordinates for advertising. Whether an advertising SDK accesses location must be established independently and disclosed before such processing takes place.

Opening directions

“Find My Car” opens directions in a maps app. This hands the destination to the selected service, which handles navigation under its own privacy practices and permissions.

Your navigation service may use a current starting location or retain activity according to its own settings. This is distinct from storing a parking record in the Application.

Copying or sharing coordinates can disclose a location to the destination you choose. Review the recipient before sharing.

4. Photos, Camera Access, and Notifications

Parking photographs

Gallery photos are selected through Android's system picker, without broad photo-library access. Selected photos are copied into private app storage. Camera access is used when taking a new parking photo.

A picker can offer content from a cloud-backed provider. Selecting such content may involve that provider retrieving the image. Adding a photo does not necessarily remove metadata already associated with it.

Revoking permission to access an original image does not necessarily delete a copy previously attached to a record.

Parking reminders

Notifications support parking-timer reminders. Their visibility depends on permission and your device's notification settings.

A notification may reveal details to someone viewing your screen. Review lock-screen previews when using reminders associated with private locations. Disabling notifications does not itself erase the underlying record.

Permission names and settings vary by Android version and device. Granting access for one feature does not authorise unrelated processing.

5. Advertising, Measurement, and Third-Party SDKs

The Application contains advertisements. Advertising processing is separate from saving your parking details. An advertising SDK may communicate directly with its provider rather than sending information through our servers.

Advertising information requiring verification

Depending on the actual SDK and configuration, advertising systems can process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from an IP address, advertising or app identifiers, device and app information, ad impressions, interactions, and diagnostics. These are categories to verify, not a statement that every category is collected by this Application.

Such information can be used to deliver ads, measure performance, prevent invalid traffic, limit repetition, or personalise advertising where implemented and legally permitted. The exact purposes must match the deployed provider's configuration.

Provider-specific disclosure remains incomplete. Before publication, identify every advertising network and mediation partner actually used, add its official privacy-policy link, and state the information each receives and its purposes.

Confirm whether SDKs access precise location, saved coordinates, photos, or parking records; whether personalised advertising is enabled; the consent and withdrawal controls actually available; and the relevant retention and deletion arrangements. No particular provider or absence of sensitive-data access is assumed.

Consent and advertising choices

Where applicable law requires consent before advertising-related device access or processing, that processing must not begin until the required consent has been obtained.

Choosing non-personalised advertising should not be described as “no data collection.” Ad delivery, security, and measurement can still involve information processing, depending on the service.

This draft does not claim that a particular privacy-options button, consent platform, or opt-out mechanism has already been implemented. The published notice must describe the actual controls and how to reopen them.

Advertisements and external destinations

Selecting an advertisement may open another website, app, or store listing. Information you independently provide there is handled according to the relevant recipient's practices. Those practices do not remove our responsibility to disclose the SDK activity within our app.

8. Storage and Retention

Local information

Local records remain until replaced or removed. Clearing app storage can erase app-specific records and attachments. Original photographs held elsewhere are separate from copies retained by the app.

Depending on app configuration, Android version, and device settings, backup or transfer services may preserve or restore local data. Backup copies can have a separate lifecycle from the installed Application.

Advertising and diagnostic records

Advertising-data retention depends on the verified provider, data category, purpose, and applicable contractual or legal requirements. This draft does not invent a fixed retention period or promise that uninstalling the app deletes advertising-provider records.

The completed provider disclosure must explain the relevant retention periods or criteria and the available deletion procedures.

Support and privacy requests

Our proposed retention rule is to keep correspondence while resolving the request and necessary follow-up. Further retention is limited to a specific continuing need, such as an unresolved dispute or legal obligation.

Information no longer needed for those purposes is to be deleted or irreversibly anonymised. A limited record of a privacy request and its outcome may be retained where necessary to demonstrate proper handling.

9. Privacy Choices and Data Deletion

Location, camera, and notifications

Review permissions in Android settings. Where supported, you can select approximate rather than precise location or revoke location access. The affected feature may become unavailable or less accurate.

Advertising settings

Supported Android devices provide advertising ID and ad-privacy controls. Depending on your version, these may allow you to reset or delete the advertising ID or change personalisation preferences.

These device controls do not necessarily remove advertisements, delete previously collected records, or replace consent controls required within the app.

Guidance: Android advertising ID controls .

Remove local records

Use Android's app-storage controls to clear the Application's storage or uninstall it. Preserve information you need before taking that action. Clearing the cache alone is not the same as clearing all app data.

Manage original files, device backups, and information already sent to navigation, advertising, or sharing services separately. Revoking a permission does not automatically erase previously stored copies.

Request deletion or exercise another right

Email rajesh@monovatech.com with the subject Parked Car finder — Privacy Request. Explain the request without sending unnecessary sensitive information.

We will handle information under our control and identify the relevant provider where a request concerns independently held records. We do not require you to create an app account to contact us.

Verification, where necessary and lawful, will be proportionate. If a retention exception prevents deletion, we will explain the relevant reason to the extent permitted by law. Uninstalling does not automatically submit a privacy request.
