Privacy Policy

0.5x Camera

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Monova Tech handles information when you use the 0.5x Camera application. The Application provides camera functionality for capturing photos and videos using your device.

Information Collection

0.5x Camera does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location.

Camera Permission

The Application requires access to your device's camera to provide its core functionality. Camera access is used to display the camera preview and capture photos or videos. Camera input is processed on your device and is not intentionally uploaded or transmitted to Monova Tech.

Photos and Videos

Photos and videos captured using 0.5x Camera may be saved to your device's storage or media library. These files remain under your control and are not intentionally uploaded to or stored on Monova Tech's servers.

Microphone Permission

If video recording with sound is supported, the Application may request microphone access. Microphone access is used only to record audio together with your videos. Audio is not intentionally uploaded or transmitted by the Application.

Device Storage

The Application may use your device's storage to save photos, videos, thumbnails, or other files required for its camera functionality. These files remain on your device unless you choose to share, move, or delete them.

Data Sharing

Monova Tech does not intentionally sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. The Application does not create a personal profile or account for you.

Data Storage and Retention

0.5x Camera does not intentionally store your personal information on Monova Tech's servers. Photos and videos created through the Application are stored locally on your device.

Children's Privacy

0.5x Camera does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the Application, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the Application. Since captured media is primarily processed and stored locally on your device, you remain responsible for securing access to your device and its stored files.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We recommend reviewing this Privacy Policy periodically.

Your Consent

By using 0.5x Camera, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
