Privacy Policy

Car Key Simulator — Remote Prank

Last updated: 21 October 2026

1. The Short Version

Car Key Simulator — Remote Prank does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal data. The app does not require an account and does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking.

The app does not request the Android INTERNET permission and cannot send data over the internet.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The app stores a small amount of information locally to remember your preferences and provide its features. This information stays on your device.

Information Storage Location Purpose
Your preferences, including selected car, punchline, volume, and theme DataStore in the app's private storage To remember your settings
Number of pranks played DataStore in the app's private storage To determine when to ask you to rate the app
Generated prank cards The app's cache directory To temporarily prepare cards for sharing; cached files are removed automatically by the system

This information is not transmitted or shared. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored app data.

3. Permissions

The app uses only the following permission:

Permission Purpose
VIBRATE To reproduce the tactile feedback of a real car key fob.

The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, or internet access.

4. Third-Party Services

The app does not include third-party analytics, crash reporting, advertising SDKs, fonts, or assets. Its sounds are generated by the app itself at runtime.

Because the app does not request internet access, it does not transmit app data to external servers.

5. Data Sharing

We do not collect or share personal information with developers, companies, advertisers, or other third parties.

If you choose to share a generated prank card using your device's sharing features, the sharing destination and any subsequent handling of that content are governed by the app or service you choose.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Preferences and the prank count remain in the app's private storage until they are changed or the app's data is cleared. Generated prank cards are kept in the cache temporarily and may be removed automatically by Android.

You can remove locally stored app data by clearing the app's data in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

7. Children's Privacy

Car Key Simulator — Remote Prank is suitable for all ages. The app does not collect personal data from users, including children.

8. Security

App preferences and prank counts are stored locally in the app's private storage. The app does not transmit this information over the internet.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes. Updates will be published in this document and in the release notes for the relevant app version. Please review this page periodically.
