Privacy Policy

Camera Zoom 1000x HD

This Privacy Policy explains how Monova Tech ("Service Provider") handles information when you use the Camera Zoom 1000x HD mobile application (the "Application").

Camera Zoom 1000x HD is designed to provide camera and zoom functionality, allowing users to capture photos and videos using their device.

Information Collection

Camera Zoom 1000x HD does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location.

In short: The Application is designed to provide camera functionality without requiring you to provide personal information to the Service Provider.

Camera Permission

The Application requires access to your device's camera to provide its core camera and zoom functionality.

Camera access is used to display the camera preview and allow you to capture photos and videos. Camera input is processed on your device to provide the requested camera functionality.

The Application does not intentionally upload or transmit your camera preview, photographs, or videos to the Service Provider.

You can manage or revoke camera permission at any time through your device settings. Revoking the permission may prevent the camera features from functioning.

Photos and Videos

Camera Zoom 1000x HD may allow you to capture and save photographs and videos to your device.

Photos and videos created using the Application are stored on your device and are used to provide the requested camera and gallery functionality.

The Application does not intentionally upload your personal photos or videos to the Service Provider.

Microphone Permission

If video recording with sound is supported, the Application may require microphone access to record audio together with your videos.

Microphone access is used only when required for video recording with audio. The Application does not intentionally upload or transmit recorded audio to the Service Provider.

Device Storage

The Application may use your device's storage to save photographs and videos that you choose to capture.

Media created by the Application remains under your control through your device's normal photo and media management features.

Data Sharing

Camera Zoom 1000x HD does not intentionally sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

The Application does not create a personal account or profile containing your personal information.

Data Storage and Retention

The Application does not intentionally store personal information on the Service Provider's servers.

Photos and videos captured using the Application are stored locally on your device according to your device's storage and media settings.

Children's Privacy

Camera Zoom 1000x HD does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to the Service Provider, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

Security

The Service Provider values your privacy and takes reasonable measures to protect information under its control.

Because the Application is designed to process camera content locally and does not intentionally transmit personal photos or videos to the Service Provider, the amount of personal information handled by the Service Provider is minimized.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by publishing an updated version of this Privacy Policy with a new effective date.

You are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

Your Consent

By using Camera Zoom 1000x HD, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Contact Us
