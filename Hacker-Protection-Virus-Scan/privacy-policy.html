Privacy Policy

Hacker Protection Virus Scan · Monova Tech

This Privacy Policy applies to the Hacker Protection Virus Scan mobile application and related services operated by Monova Tech (collectively, the "Application"). Monova Tech is referred to as the "Service Provider" throughout this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

The Application is designed to perform security checks, scans, audits, and data processing locally on your device. We do not operate our own backend servers for these features.

Depending on the features you use, the Application accesses information on your device, including:

  • Installed application names, package identifiers, permissions, services, installer information, and relevant security settings.
  • Files that you choose to scan for potential secrets, sensitive information, or unnecessary data.
  • Clipboard changes observed while the Application's clipboard protection functionality is active.
  • Wi-Fi network details, including network name (SSID), BSSID, security information, signal strength, and network diagnostics.
  • Device security information, such as root status, SELinux status, developer options, screen-lock status, encryption, security patch level, VPN status, and related indicators.
  • Network connection information used to identify connections and associate them with applications where possible.

This information is processed to provide the Application's security, auditing, monitoring, and maintenance features. Except for the public IP lookup described below and potential Android backup or transfer processes, this processing takes place on your device.

2. Network Access and Public IP Address

The Application makes an external request to api.ipify.org to retrieve your public IP address for display in Wi-Fi Guard.

When this request is made, your public IP address is necessarily visible to the external service. The service may also receive ordinary connection metadata associated with the request. The Application displays the returned public IP address on your device and does not intentionally save it in its own persistent application storage.

The public IP lookup is the Application's external data request for this feature. We do not intentionally transmit your scanned files, clipboard contents, vault credentials, or scan reports to our own servers.

Please review the external service's privacy practices for information about how it handles requests: ipify.

3. Local Scanning and Security Audits

The Application performs supported scans and security audits on your device. These may include installed application auditing, file and secret scanning, junk-file identification, APK inspection, connection monitoring, and device security checks.

Files and information inspected during these operations are processed locally. Scan findings are generally held in memory while the scan runs. Certain findings, including threat-related package names and file paths, may be included in a locally stored scan report.

Security checks have technical limitations. Results may be incomplete or inaccurate, and the Application cannot guarantee detection of every malicious application, vulnerability, exposed secret, or other security threat. Results should not be considered a guarantee of complete device protection.

4. Clipboard Protection

When clipboard protection is active and Android allows the Application to observe clipboard changes, clipboard content may be classified locally to identify potentially sensitive information, such as payment card details, keys, one-time passwords, or recovery phrases.

The Application stores a limited history of up to 30 clipboard events in local application preferences. Clipboard history entries use masked previews intended to avoid retaining the complete sensitive value. The Application may also provide an optional feature to clear clipboard contents automatically.

Clipboard access is subject to Android's operating system restrictions and application lifecycle. Background clipboard access may be restricted on supported Android versions. The Application does not guarantee continuous observation of clipboard changes.

You can manage the available clipboard protection features within the Application. Clipboard history is removed when you use the Reset All Data option.

5. Password Vault and Encryption

The Application provides a local vault for credentials that you choose to save. Vault data is stored on your device in an application-private file and is encrypted using AES-256-GCM with a key derived using PBKDF2 and 150,000 iterations.

Vault credentials and the master password are not intentionally transmitted to the Service Provider or an external server by the vault functionality. You may copy a saved password to your clipboard, in which case clipboard behavior and protection settings apply.

Encryption reduces the risk of unauthorized access, but no security mechanism is infallible. Keep your credentials and master password secure.

6. Information Stored on Your Device

The Application stores certain information locally to provide its features and preserve your settings. This may include:

  • Scan reports containing selected threat details, such as relevant package names or file paths.
  • Masked clipboard event history.
  • Encrypted vault data.
  • Scan history, dashboard counters, and security statistics.
  • Application preferences and settings.

These records are stored in application-private storage or local preferences. They are not intentionally uploaded to a server operated by Monova Tech.

7. Android Backup and Device Transfers

Android's backup and device-transfer functionality may copy eligible application data to a backup destination or another device, depending on the Android version, device settings, and applicable backup rules.

If Android backup is enabled and the relevant files or preferences are eligible for backup, some locally stored information may be included. This could include application preferences, scan history, clipboard records, or vault data, depending on the backup configuration.

Backup and transfer processing is governed by the applicable Android platform and backup service settings. You can review your device's backup settings to understand the available controls. Local storage alone should not be interpreted as a guarantee that data can never leave the device.

8. Location Permission and Wi-Fi Information

Android may require location permission for an application to access certain Wi-Fi network details, including SSID and BSSID.

The Application uses this permission for Wi-Fi-related functionality. It does not use this permission to determine, store, or transmit your geographical coordinates as part of that functionality.

Wi-Fi network information is displayed or processed locally and is not intentionally saved as a persistent network history. The public IP lookup is described separately in Section 2.

9. Permissions and Their Purposes

Depending on the Android version and the features you use, the Application may request the following permissions or special access:

  • Installed application visibility: Used to inspect installed applications and their relevant permissions and security-related properties.
  • All Files Access or storage permissions: Used by supported file scanning and junk-cleaning features to inspect eligible files and identify items you may wish to remove.
  • Location permission: Used where required by Android to access Wi-Fi SSID, BSSID, and related network details, not for geographical location tracking.
  • Internet and network permissions: Used to retrieve the public IP address and access relevant network state information.
  • Background process management: Used by the Booster feature where supported by Android.
  • Notifications and foreground service permissions: Used to support the Shield's ongoing protection features and show relevant notifications.
  • Boot receiver and wake lock: Used to support configured protection behavior after device startup and keep supported operations active.
  • Battery optimization exemption: May be requested to help supported background protection features operate reliably.
  • Device administrator: Used for the SOS force-lock feature. This access is not used by the feature to wipe your device or impose password policies.
  • Vibration: Used for the SOS siren feature.

You can manage available permissions and special access through Android settings. Some features may not function when the necessary access is denied or revoked.

10. Advertising, Analytics, and Third-Party Services

The Application's current implementation does not include analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, or an application backend. It uses AndroidX and Material libraries for its application functionality.

The external public IP lookup described in Section 2 is provided by ipify. This external service is not operated by Monova Tech and is subject to its own privacy practices.

If additional third-party services or advertising SDKs are introduced in a future version, this policy will be updated as appropriate to explain their data practices.

11. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Monova Tech does not operate a backend server for collecting scan reports, vault credentials, clipboard history, or other locally processed security information. We do not intentionally sell your personal information.

Information may nevertheless be processed by external services when you use features that communicate with them, as described in this policy. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, or security.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

Most information generated by the Application is stored locally on your device. The Application does not maintain its own server-side database of your scan history, clipboard records, vault credentials, or security statistics.

You can remove locally stored application data using Settings → Reset All Data. This feature is designed to clear the counters, scan history, clipboard log, and vault data maintained by the Application.

Uninstalling the Application removes its application-private local data under normal Android behavior. However, data already included in Android backups or transferred to another device may remain in those destinations until separately removed according to their applicable settings and policies.

The Application does not control retention practices of external services such as ipify or Android backup providers. Refer to those services for information about their respective data handling and retention practices.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to the Service Provider, contact us using the email address below so that the matter can be reviewed and appropriate action taken.

You are responsible for keeping your device secure, using a strong screen lock, protecting your vault credentials, and reviewing the permissions granted to the Application.

15. International Data Transfers

The Application does not operate a backend for transferring your locally processed scan data to Monova Tech. However, the public IP lookup and eligible Android backup or device transfer features may involve services operated outside your country of residence.

Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers of personal data, the relevant service provider is responsible for implementing the required safeguards for the processing it performs.
