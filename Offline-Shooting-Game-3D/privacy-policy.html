1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to Offline Shooting Game 3D (the “Game”), operated by Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. (“we,” “us,” “our,” or the “Service Provider”).

It addresses information handled through gameplay, advertising, integrated services, and communications with us. It distinguishes on-device processing from information transmitted to the Service Provider or another organisation.

Where applicable law uses the term “data controller,” we act as controller for processing whose purposes and means we determine. An external provider's role depends on the actual processing and arrangements with that provider.

2. Gameplay Information

Game state and player input

The Game processes your input and the current game state to respond to actions such as movement, aiming, firing, pausing, restarting, and changing supported settings.

Depending on the features available in the installed version, saved game information can include progress, scores, selections, and preferences. Where these records are stored locally, they support restoring your progress and settings on the device.

Local saving does not establish that no gameplay events are transmitted by an advertising or analytics component. Any such transmission must be identified separately in the service disclosures below.

Device characteristics

The Game uses the device capabilities needed to render its graphics and respond to input. Display dimensions, orientation, and graphics capabilities can be relevant to that operation.

Information used locally to render the Game is distinct from device information sent with an advertising request. This policy does not describe all device information as anonymous or local-only.

3. Device Access and Permissions

Review the Game's available permissions through Android settings. The controls and menu names vary by Android version and device manufacturer.

Playing game sound is different from recording microphone audio. Similarly, an in-game viewpoint does not, by itself, require access to the device's physical camera.

The exact permissions requested by the released Game and its SDKs must be verified. This draft does not establish that microphone, location, media, or other sensitive access is present or absent.

An advertising identifier and location inferred from an IP address are also different from precise location obtained through a location permission. Advertising processing must not be overlooked simply because no sensitive permission prompt appears.

Where permission or a prominent disclosure is required, it must relate to the actual feature and processing involved. Permission for one function does not authorise unrelated collection.

4. Information You Send to Us

If you contact us for support, feedback, or a privacy request, we receive the information included in your message. This can include your email address, any name you provide, message content, attachments, and ordinary email delivery information.

You may voluntarily provide your device model, Android version, Game version, or details about a gameplay problem to help us respond.

Remove unrelated notifications, account details, and private information from screenshots before sending them. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or unnecessary identity documents.

5. Advertising and External Services

Advertisements in the Game

Offline Shooting Game 3D contains ads. Advertising is part of the current Game, not merely a possible future feature.

Advertising services can receive information directly through software integrated into the Game. Such processing remains relevant to this policy even when the Service Provider does not receive a copy through its own servers.

Advertising information and purposes

The information processed depends on the actual advertising integration, its configuration, your choices, and applicable requirements. Categories that must be checked include:

  • IP addresses and approximate location derived from those addresses.
  • Advertising identifiers and other device, app, or installation identifiers used by the deployed services.
  • Device and app information, such as operating-system version and diagnostic information.
  • Ad requests, impressions, interactions, and measurement events.
  • Consent or privacy-choice records where those are processed by the integration.

These are review categories, not a verified inventory of this Game's collection. Potential purposes include delivering advertisements, measuring performance, detecting invalid traffic, and personalising ads where implemented and legally permitted.

Provider-specific disclosure to complete: Identify the actual advertising network, every relevant mediation partner, and any analytics or crash-reporting provider. Include their official privacy links, the information each receives, and its purposes.

Also specify the actual consent and opt-out controls, how users reopen those controls, retention and deletion arrangements, processing locations, and any child-directed configuration. No particular advertising company is assumed.

Personalised and non-personalised advertising

Where consent is required for advertising-related device access or processing, the activity must not begin before valid consent is obtained. This draft does not assert that a consent platform or privacy-options screen has already been implemented.

Non-personalised advertising does not necessarily mean that no information is processed. Ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention can still involve data, depending on the service and configuration.

Offline availability

Offline gameplay does not guarantee that advertisements or related optional functions work without a connection. Advertising availability depends on the integration and network conditions.

Being offline does not delete information already transmitted to an advertising service and is not a substitute for withdrawing consent or exercising an applicable privacy right.

External destinations

Selecting an advertisement may open a website, another app, or an app-store page. Information you provide there is handled by that recipient. Its privacy notice does not replace our responsibility to explain advertising processing within this Game.

Platform services and this policy page

Android, Google Play, and your device manufacturer may independently process information for installation, updates, security, diagnostics, or backups. An SDK integrated into the Game is not independent platform processing merely because another company operates it.

This HTML document contains no scripts, tracking pixels, embedded ads, or externally loaded fonts. Its hosting service may separately process connection information and server logs.

Local records

Records saved in app-specific storage remain subject to the Game's save behaviour and your device's storage controls. Clearing app data can remove saved progress and preferences. Restarting a level is not necessarily the same as deleting saved data.

Advertising and diagnostic information

Retention must be specified for the providers and data actually used. The completed disclosure should state retention periods or meaningful criteria, including any justified fraud-prevention, accounting, security, or legal exceptions.

No fixed advertising-data retention period has been verified for this Game. Uninstalling it does not automatically delete records already held by an advertising provider.

Support and privacy correspondence

The proposed retention rule for correspondence is to keep identifiable information while resolving the request and necessary follow-up. Further retention should be limited to a specific continuing need, such as an unresolved dispute or applicable legal obligation.

Information no longer needed for those purposes should be deleted or irreversibly anonymised. A limited record of a privacy request and its outcome may be retained where necessary to demonstrate appropriate handling.

9. Privacy Choices and Data Deletion

Android advertising controls

Supported Android devices provide controls to reset or delete the advertising ID. Available menus vary by device and version. Older versions may provide an option to limit personalised advertising.

These controls do not necessarily remove advertisements, delete previously collected records, or replace consent and opt-out mechanisms required within the Game.

Official guidance: Android advertising ID controls .

Remove local game information

Use Android's app-storage controls to clear app data or uninstall the Game. Clearing app data can remove saved progress and preferences, so preserve information you need before taking that action.

Clearing only the cache is not necessarily the same as clearing all app data. Manage backup copies and information held by external services separately.
