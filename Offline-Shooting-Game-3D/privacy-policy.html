1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to Offline Shooting Game 3D (the “Game”), operated by Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. (“we,” “us,” “our,” or the “Service Provider”).

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Game and when you communicate with us. It distinguishes information processed locally on your device from personal information you choose to provide directly to us.

Where applicable privacy law uses the term “data controller,” Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. acts as controller for personal information that we receive and determine how to use, including support and privacy communications.

2. Gameplay Information

Game progress

The Game processes information necessary to provide gameplay. Depending on your progress, this may include completed or unlocked levels, mission progress, scores, achievements, earned in-game rewards, selected equipment, gameplay settings, and other information needed to restore your current game state.

This information is stored locally on your device for the offline gameplay covered by this policy. It is used so that you can continue playing without starting again each time the Game is opened.

Gameplay actions

The Game processes your interactions with its controls, such as movement, aiming, firing, selecting levels, changing equipment, pausing, restarting, and adjusting settings. These actions are processed to respond to your input and operate the game.

The Game's simulated weapons, targets, missions, characters, and shooting actions are virtual gameplay elements. They do not represent collection of information about real-world weapons, shooting activity, or your physical movements outside the Game.

Settings and preferences

The Game may store preferences such as sound, music, vibration, sensitivity, graphics quality, control configuration, and other supported gameplay options. These settings are retained locally so that your preferences can be restored when you return to the Game.

Device information used locally

The Game may use technical characteristics of your device, such as display size, orientation, graphics capabilities, and available resources, to render the 3D environment and provide compatible gameplay.

Processing technical characteristics on the device does not, by itself, mean that those characteristics are transmitted to the Service Provider.

3. Device Access and Permissions

Core offline gameplay does not require access to your contacts, call history, text messages, precise location, personal photo library, or personal files.

Audio

The Game may play music, sound effects, and other game audio through your device. Playing game audio is different from recording microphone audio. The offline gameplay described in this policy does not require recording your conversations or surrounding sounds.

Vibration

Where vibration or haptic feedback is supported, the Game may activate your device's vibration feature in response to gameplay events. This provides tactile feedback and does not collect personal information.

Permissions

You can review permissions available to the Game through your device settings. The names and locations of permission controls can vary depending on the Android version and device manufacturer.

If a future Game feature requires additional sensitive access, the relevant permission and privacy disclosures will be provided as required. Granting access for one feature does not authorise unrelated processing.

4. Information You Send to Us

You do not need to provide your name, email address, telephone number, or other direct contact information to play the offline Game.

If you choose to contact us for support, feedback, or a privacy request, we receive the information contained in your communication. This may include your email address, name if you provide one, message content, attachments, and ordinary email delivery information.

You may voluntarily provide information such as your device model, Android version, Game version, level or mission concerned, or a description of a technical problem to help us investigate your request.

Screenshots can contain notifications, account names, or information from outside the Game. Remove unrelated private information before sending screenshots or other attachments.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, identity documents, or other sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request.

5. Advertising, Analytics, and External Services

Offline gameplay

The core gameplay described by this policy is designed to function without an internet connection. Game progress and settings described above are maintained locally rather than through a developer-operated player account.

Offline functionality does not mean that every service on your device is offline. Android, Google Play, your device manufacturer, backup services, or other platform components may independently communicate with their providers under their own privacy practices.

Advertising and analytics

This policy does not identify an advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting provider because no such provider has been confirmed for the Game in the information used to prepare this policy.

If the distributed Game includes advertising, analytics, crash reporting, attribution, or another third-party SDK that transmits information, the relevant provider, information categories, purposes, consent controls, and applicable privacy links must be added to this policy before those statements can accurately describe that release.

App store and operating system

Google Play, Android, and your device manufacturer may process information independently for app installation, updates, security, platform diagnostics, licensing, backups, or account services.

Their independent processing is distinct from information collected by the Game itself. An SDK integrated directly into the Game is not considered independent merely because another company operates that SDK.

This privacy-policy page

This HTML document does not contain JavaScript, advertising, tracking pixels, externally loaded fonts, or embedded third-party media.

The web server used to host this page may separately process ordinary connection information, such as an IP address, browser information, and request time. The absence of tracking code in this document does not establish that its hosting service maintains no server logs.

Local gameplay information is used to:

  • Operate levels, missions, and other gameplay;
  • Respond to your controls and in-game actions;
  • Save and restore supported game progress
