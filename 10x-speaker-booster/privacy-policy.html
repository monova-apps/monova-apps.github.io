1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to 10x Speaker sound booster (the “Application”), an Android application operated by Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. (“we,” “us,” “our,” or the “Service Provider”). It explains how information is handled when you use the Application, view or interact with advertisements, or contact us.

This policy distinguishes information stored locally on your device, information processed through advertising services, and information you provide directly to us. Our contact details appear in Section 14.

2. Information and Audio Processing

On-device audio operation

Under the audio configuration described in this policy, audio adjustment takes place on your device. The Application does not create microphone recordings, record conversations, upload playback audio, or send audio files to Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. for processing.

Information needed to apply an audio setting is used for the requested audio operation. Audio content is not provided to advertising services under this configuration.

Local preferences

The Application stores preferences such as your selected boost level and interface settings locally so that it can remember your choices. These preferences are not automatically sent to us. Device backup or migration services may handle app data separately according to your device settings and the service provider's practices.

Advertising information

The Application includes third-party advertising technology. Depending on the advertising provider, SDK configuration, device settings, and applicable consent, advertising services may automatically collect or receive:

  • Device and identifier information: advertising identifiers or other app or device identifiers, device model, operating-system version, language, and relevant technical settings.
  • Network information: IP address and connection information. An IP address may be used to estimate your general location; this differs from accessing precise GPS location.
  • Advertising and interaction information: ad requests, impressions, clicks, video views, and interactions with advertisements or the Application that the advertising SDK measures.
  • Diagnostic information: advertising SDK errors, crash information, performance measurements, and related technical data.
  • Privacy preferences: consent status and signals used to apply advertising choices and legal restrictions.

These categories describe potential advertising processing rather than confirmation that every listed item is collected by every provider. Advertising information may be transmitted to provider servers even though the Application's audio processing takes place locally.

Information you send to us

If you email us, we receive your email address, the message, and any information or attachments you include. You may choose to provide your name, device model, Android version, app version, screenshots, or a description of a problem. We use this information to respond to you and handle your request.

Accounts and measurement

The configuration described here has no developer account system. However, the absence of an account does not mean that advertising services collect no information. Advertising SDKs may perform measurement and diagnostics as part of their operation.
