1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to 10x Speaker sound booster (the “Application”), an Android application operated by Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. (“we,” “us,” “our,” or the “Service Provider”). It describes the privacy terms for the configuration identified above, including on-device information handling and communications with us.

The Application's purpose is to let you adjust supported audio output on your Android device. This policy distinguishes information handled locally by the Application from information you choose to provide directly to us.

Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. is responsible for personal information it receives and controls in connection with support and privacy requests. Our contact details appear in Section 14.

2. Information and Audio Processing

On-device audio operation

In the configuration covered by this policy, audio adjustment takes place on your device. The Application does not create microphone recordings, record conversations, upload playback audio, or send audio files to Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. for processing.

Information needed to apply an audio setting is used for the requested audio operation. It is not used to create a listening-history profile or identify the contents of your conversations.

Local preferences

The Application stores preferences such as your selected boost level and interface settings locally so that it can remember your choices. These preferences are not automatically sent to us. Device backup or migration services may handle app data separately according to your device settings and the service provider's practices.

Information you send to us

If you email us, we receive your email address, the message, and any information or attachments you include. You may choose to provide your name, device model, Android version, app version, screenshots, or a description of a problem. We use this information to respond to you and handle your request.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, recordings of other people, or unnecessary sensitive information with a support request. We do not need your complete device contents to investigate a routine issue.

Accounts and automatic tracking

The configuration covered here does not use a developer account system or automatically send advertising identifiers, usage events, or crash reports to us or to embedded analytics or advertising services. Diagnostic details sent voluntarily with a support message are covered by the support provisions of this policy.

3. Android Permissions and Device Controls
