1. Who We Are and What This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy describes how Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. ("we," "us," "our," or the "Service Provider") handles information in connection with Moon Shot Camera 8k (the "Application") and communications about the Application.

The policy applies to the mobile versions distributed by us that use the configuration described above. It does not state that the Application is available on a particular app store. It distinguishes information processed locally on your device from information you choose to send to us.

Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd., located in Nepal, is responsible for personal information that we receive and determine how to use, including support correspondence and privacy requests. Our contact details appear in Section 14.

2. Information We Access or Receive

Camera access and photographs

The Application accesses your device's camera when you authorize access and use a camera feature. Camera preview data and photographs are processed on your device to provide the capture, display, and saving functions you request. We do not automatically transmit your camera feed or photographs to company servers.

Where a photo-selection or editing function is provided, the Application accesses the images you select or the photo-library access you authorize. That access is used for the requested function, not to collect your entire library for unrelated purposes. Images you save remain in the local app storage or photo-library destination used by the feature.

Image metadata and local settings

Image files can contain metadata such as capture time, dimensions, orientation, and camera settings. An image imported from another source may already contain additional metadata, including location. Do not assume that editing or sharing an image removes its metadata.

The Application stores camera preferences and other necessary settings locally to remember your choices. It can read relevant camera capabilities, device orientation, and operating-system information locally to operate compatible features. These local settings and technical readings are not sent to us as usage analytics.

Information you send to us

If you email us, we receive your email address, the name you include, message contents, attachments, and technical message information provided through the email system. You may choose to supply a device model, operating-system version, app version, or screenshot when explaining an issue. We use that information to handle your inquiry.

Please do not include passwords, payment-card details, identity documents, or unrelated sensitive photographs in an initial support request. If an attachment contains another person's information, share it only when appropriate and necessary.

Information outside this release's processing

The release covered by this policy does not create user accounts, record microphone audio, access precise device location, collect contacts or messages, collect advertising identifiers, or send automatic usage or crash reports to us. It does not create biometric identity templates, identify people from their faces, send photographs to a remote AI service, or use your photographs to train AI models.

3. How We Use Information

Local camera and image information is used to perform the photography functions you request. Local preferences are used to remember settings. Technical device information processed locally is used to select supported camera behavior and display the interface appropriately.

Information you send directly to us is used to answer your questions, investigate the issue you report, handle privacy requests, protect the integrity of our communications, and meet applicable legal obligations. Sending a support inquiry does not subscribe you to marketing messages.

Where a data-protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on the basis appropriate to the specific activity: providing a service you request where processing is necessary for that service; our legitimate interests in responding to inquiries, resolving reported problems, and protecting our services where those interests are not overridden by your rights; compliance with legal duties; or consent where required.

A device permission authorizes the corresponding device access; it is not blanket consent for unrelated data collection. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it. Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal.

4. Permissions and Device Controls

Camera: Camera access enables the preview and photo-capture functions. Declining access prevents camera-dependent features from operating.

Photos or media: Access is used to open selected images or save photographs where the relevant feature and operating system require it. The scope of access depends on the permission or system picker you authorize.

You can review or revoke available permissions in your device's application or privacy settings. Menu names and available choices vary between Android and iOS versions. Revoking access may disable the associated feature and does not by itself delete images already saved or shared.

This release does not require microphone or precise-location access. The Application does not treat an operating-system permission as permission to use images for advertising or other unrelated purposes.

Android guidance: Change app permissions.

5. Sharing, Third Parties, and Service Providers

Sharing you initiate: If you export or share a photograph using a sharing function provided by your device or another application, the recipient receives the selected content and any metadata included in the file. That recipient's handling of its copy is subject to its own practices. We cannot recall a copy you have already shared.

Device backups and synchronization: A photo-library or device-backup service you enable can copy saved content or eligible app data to its own storage. This is separate from an automatic upload to Monova Tech Pvt. Ltd. Backup availability, storage, and deletion depend on your device settings and the service involved.

Communications providers: Email-delivery, storage, security, and related business-service providers process information necessary to deliver and maintain communications you send us. We limit our use and disclosure of that correspondence to the stated purposes and require appropriate protection where a provider processes information on our behalf.

Legal and protective disclosures: We may disclose information we hold when necessary and legally permitted to comply with a binding legal obligation or valid legal process, protect people's safety, investigate unlawful activity, or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Business changes: A merger, acquisition, reorganization, or transfer of business assets may involve relevant business records, including support correspondence. Any transfer remains subject to applicable privacy requirements and required notices. It does not give us access to photographs that exist only on your device.

Android backup guidance: Back up or restore device data.

6. Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

The release covered by this policy does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not use the Application for cross-app tracking, behavioral advertising, or building advertising profiles. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

The Application does not use cookies for tracking. An external website, email service, app store, or sharing service you separately visit may process information under its own privacy notice. That separate processing does not establish that the Application integrates the service's tracking technology.

Introducing advertising, analytics, cloud image processing, or additional data collection would require a policy update reflecting the actual providers, information, purposes, and controls, together with any necessary notices and consent before the new processing begins.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Photographs and local preferences: These remain on your device until you delete them, clear the relevant app data, or remove the relevant local storage. Media saved outside the app's private storage may remain after the app is removed. Copies held by your photo or backup provider follow that provider's retention and deletion procedures.

Temporary processing data: Camera and image buffers are used for the active operation. Temporary files and cached previews are retained only as needed for processing, recovery of an interrupted operation, or local performance, and are subject to local cache clearing. We do not maintain a server-side archive of these buffers or local files.

Support correspondence: We retain messages and necessary attachments while addressing the inquiry and for as long as reasonably necessary for relevant follow-up, resolution of a dispute, or applicable legal obligations. Information that is no longer necessary for those purposes is deleted or irreversibly anonymized.

Privacy-request records: We retain the minimum record needed to handle and demonstrate our response and satisfy relevant legal duties. If information must be retained after a deletion request, we restrict its use to the justification for retention and explain the exception to the extent legally permitted.

8. Deleting Information and Stopping Processing

You can stop using the Application and revoke its camera or photo permissions. Delete saved photographs through the storage or photo-library application that holds them. Review any trash, recently deleted folder, synchronized copy, or backup separately; deleting one copy does not necessarily remove every copy.

On Android, the available app-storage settings can be used to clear local app data. On other supported devices, use the relevant device controls to delete app data or remove the app. Uninstallation does not send us a request to delete correspondence already held by us.

To request deletion of information you sent us, email rajesh@monovatech.com with the subject Moon Shot Camera 8k — Data Deletion. Describe the information or correspondence involved. We may request limited additional information where necessary and lawful to verify the request.

We cannot remotely delete photographs we do not hold from your device, another person's device, or an independent backup service. This release has no app accounts to delete; an app-store account is managed separately by its provider.

Children's personal information requires particular care. We do not use the Application to profile children for advertising or ask users to submit children's personal information to us as part of its camera functions.
