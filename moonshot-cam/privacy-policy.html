2. Information We Access or Receive

Camera access and photographs

The Application accesses your camera when you authorize access and use a camera feature. Under the camera configuration described here, preview data and photographs are processed on your device for the capture, display, and saving functions you request.

We do not automatically transmit your camera feed or photographs to company servers under this configuration.

Where photo-selection or editing is provided, the Application accesses images you select or the photo-library access you authorize. Access is used for the requested function rather than collecting your entire library for unrelated purposes.

Saved images remain in the app-storage or photo-library destination used by the feature. Under this configuration, camera previews and photographs are not provided to advertising services for advertising purposes.

Image metadata and local settings

Images may contain capture time, dimensions, orientation, and camera settings. Imported images may already contain additional metadata, including location. Editing or sharing an image does not necessarily remove its metadata.

The Application stores camera preferences and necessary settings locally. It may read camera capabilities, device orientation, and operating-system information to operate compatible features. These local settings are not automatically sent to us as usage analytics under the described configuration.

Advertising-related information

Advertising technology integrated into the Application may automatically collect or receive the following categories, depending on the provider, platform, SDK configuration, device settings, and applicable consent:

  • Identifiers: advertising identifiers or other app or device identifiers supported by the provider and permitted on the platform.
  • Device and application information: device model, operating-system version, app version, language, and relevant technical settings.
  • Network information: IP address and connection information. An IP address may be used to estimate general location without accessing precise GPS location.
  • Advertising and interaction information: ad requests, impressions, clicks, video views, and application interactions measured by the advertising SDK.
  • Diagnostics: advertising SDK errors, crash information, performance measurements, and related technical data.
  • Privacy preferences: consent status and signals used to apply advertising choices and legal restrictions.

These are potential categories rather than confirmation that every listed item is collected by every provider. Advertising information may be transmitted to provider servers separately from local camera processing.

Information you send to us

If you email us, we receive your email address, any name you include, message contents, attachments, and technical message information provided by the email system. You may supply a device model, operating-system version, app version, or screenshot when reporting a problem.

Do not include passwords, payment-card details, identity documents, or unrelated sensitive photographs in an initial request. Share another person's information only where appropriate and necessary.

Other information handling

The described feature configuration does not create app accounts, record microphone audio, access precise device location, or collect contacts or messages. It does not create biometric identity templates, identify people from faces, send photographs to a remote AI service, or use photographs to train AI models.

These feature limitations do not exclude advertising identifiers, ad measurement, or advertising SDK diagnostics described above.
