Granting camera permission is not consent to unrelated advertising use, cloud uploads, or microphone recording. Each activity must be explained according to its actual purpose and applicable requirements.

Photos and other device features

Android's system photo picker can grant access to selected images without granting access to an entire photo library. The actual access method depends on the Application's implementation and Android version.

Selected images may come from a cloud-backed provider. That provider can retrieve the selected content under its own arrangements. This is distinct from an upload performed by the Application.
