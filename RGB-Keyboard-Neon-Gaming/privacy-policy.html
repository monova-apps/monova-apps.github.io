2. Keyboard Input and Sensitive Information

When enabled and selected, the Application processes key presses, composed text, and editing commands and delivers input to the active app and field through Android.

Android may make limited editor information available, such as field type, cursor position, selected or surrounding text, and the identity of the app requesting input. Access depends on Android and the receiving editor. It does not provide unrestricted access to all apps, game activity, messages, or historical contents.

We do not retain a complete typing history, upload typed content or surrounding text, or provide them to advertising services. A game, chat service, or other app receiving your input handles that text under its own privacy practices.

Keyboards necessarily handle entered characters, potentially including account credentials, payment details, or private messages. We do not separately collect or retain this content as a record. Sensitive-field behaviour also depends on how the receiving app marks its fields; this policy does not guarantee that the input method is technically unable to encounter sensitive content.

3. Local Preferences and Optional Features

Theme, RGB colour, supported animation, layout, language, keyboard size, sound, vibration, and other preferences may be saved locally. The gaming-inspired appearance does not require access to game accounts, gameplay recordings, or your complete installed-app inventory.

If suggestions or a personal dictionary are included, processing and supported learning remain on-device. The workflow described here does not use cloud prediction. Learned words may contain personal information and can be removed with available reset controls or app-data deletion.

If clipboard features are available, clipboard text is processed for actions you request under Android’s restrictions. Supported saved clips remain local until removed. Clipboard contents are not supplied to advertisers.

If custom backgrounds are supported, selected images are used locally for keyboard personalisation. App-specific copies may persist until removed; originals have a separate storage lifecycle.

4. Permissions and Activation

You enable and select the keyboard through Android input-method settings. Installation alone does not activate it. Android may display a general warning about third-party keyboard access.

Android’s BIND_INPUT_METHOD protection controls connections to the keyboard service. It is not broad permission to access contacts, messages, game accounts, or unrelated files.

Network and advertising-related access supports AdMob. An image picker or media permission may support an included custom-background feature. The typing workflow does not require camera access, microphone recording, contacts, SMS, call history, or precise GPS. A separate voice-input service, if invoked, follows its provider’s permissions and policy.
