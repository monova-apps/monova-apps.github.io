2. Keyboard Input and Sensitive Information

When enabled and selected, the Application functions as an Android input method. It processes key presses and composed text and sends the resulting input to the app and text field you are using.

Android may also make limited information about the active editor available, such as input type, cursor position, selected text, surrounding text, and the identity of the app requesting input. Availability depends on Android and the receiving app. This access supports typing and does not provide unrestricted access to every app or its history.

We do not create a persistent history of everything you type, upload messages or surrounding text, or disclose keyboard input to advertisers. Text you enter is delivered to the destination app, whose privacy practices govern its subsequent use.

A keyboard necessarily handles characters you enter, potentially including passwords, payment information, or private messages. We do not separately collect or retain such content as a record. Password-field behaviour depends partly on how the destination app identifies the field; this policy does not guarantee that sensitive content is technically inaccessible to the input method.

3. Local Preferences and Optional Features

Theme selection, keyboard size, language or layout choices, sound, vibration, and other available preferences may be stored locally.

If suggestions or a personal dictionary are available, ordinary word processing and any supported learning remain on-device. The Application does not use a cloud prediction service. Locally learned words may themselves contain personal information. Use available reset controls or clear app data to remove them.

If clipboard features are included, clipboard text is accessed for a paste or clipboard action you request, subject to Android restrictions. Any supported pinned or saved clips remain local until removed through available controls or app-data deletion. Clipboard content is not supplied to advertising services.

If a custom-background feature is available, images you select are used locally to personalise the keyboard. A working copy may remain in app storage. Deleting that copy does not necessarily delete the original image.

4. Permissions and Keyboard Activation

Installing the Application does not automatically select it as your keyboard. You enable and choose it through Android input-method settings. Android may display a general warning about the access third-party keyboards can have.

The keyboard service is protected by Android’s BIND_INPUT_METHOD mechanism. This system integration is different from an ordinary runtime permission and does not grant unrelated access to contacts, messages, or files.

Network and advertising-related access supports AdMob. A system image picker or media permission may be used only where an included custom-background feature needs it. The typing workflow described here does not require camera, microphone recording, contacts, SMS, call history, or precise GPS. If you invoke a separate voice-input service, its provider’s permissions and privacy policy apply.
